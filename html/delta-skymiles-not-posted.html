SkyMiles Not Posted After Your Delta Flight — What to Do

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to file a missing mileage request directly with a Delta specialist — the fastest way to recover SkyMiles that failed to post after your flight. Under normal circumstances, SkyMiles appear in your account within 72 hours of a completed trip. When they do not show up within five business days, the cause is usually a name mismatch, a missing frequent flyer number on the booking, or a partner airline reporting delay. This guide walks through each scenario and the exact steps to get your miles credited.

Why SkyMiles Sometimes Don't Post Automatically

Delta's mileage system credits SkyMiles when the name on your ticket exactly matches the name registered on your SkyMiles profile. A single-character difference — a middle initial included in one but not the other, a hyphenated surname entered differently, or a nickname in place of a legal name — is enough to prevent automatic posting.

Other common causes include flights booked without a SkyMiles number attached, flights where you checked in as a guest and linked your frequent flyer number at the gate (which can delay posting by an additional 24–48 hours), or promotional mileage offers that operate on a separate timeline of up to eight weeks after the qualifying activity.

Flights operated by Delta's partner carriers — Air France, KLM, Virgin Atlantic, Korean Air, and other SkyTeam members — are particularly prone to delays. Mileage reporting on partner flights is controlled by the operating airline, not Delta, and can take four to six weeks. When a credit finally posts for a partner flight, it may appear as two separate line items: base miles and a separate bonus miles entry.

Another subtlety worth understanding is the difference between a codeshare flight and a true partner-operated flight. On a codeshare, Delta assigns a DL flight number to a route operated by another carrier. Mileage credit on codeshares depends on which airline's code is on your ticket at check-in — if you checked in under a Delta DL number, Delta's system handles the posting; if your boarding pass showed a partner code, the credit flows through the partner's reporting channel and may take several additional business days. Reviewing your boarding pass and original ticket carefully helps identify which posting path applies.

How to Request a Missing Mileage Credit from Delta

Delta accepts retroactive mileage claims for flights completed within the past six months. To submit online, sign in to delta.com, go to "My Account," and select "Request Missing Miles" under the Travel section. The form asks for your flight number, travel date, departure and arrival cities, and booking confirmation number.

The online form handles most Delta-operated flights efficiently. For partner airline credits, Delta's system may redirect you to submit through the partner carrier's portal — or you can call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and have an agent submit the partner credit request directly on your behalf, which is often faster than navigating multiple websites.

After submitting, Delta processes most online requests within four to six business days and sends a confirmation email with a case number. Use that case number for any follow-up. Retroactive claims for flights older than six months require manual review and are handled individually by the SkyMiles service team — approval is not guaranteed but is possible with documentation.

When submitting a retroactive mileage claim online, Delta's system asks for your flight number and travel date. If either entry does not match Delta's flight records exactly — for example, a flight that was renumbered due to a schedule change — the form returns an error rather than posting the credit. In those situations, calling directly and providing the original booking confirmation number allows an agent to search by reservation record rather than relying on the flight number, which typically bypasses the matching issue and routes the credit through a manual review process instead.

Need help right now? Call Delta support at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 — specialists available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Checking Your Account for Pending Miles

Before filing a claim, confirm the miles are truly absent rather than simply processing. Sign in to your SkyMiles account and open the "Activity" tab. Pending miles appear in a lighter style and are real credits that have not yet converted to spendable SkyMiles — typically within one to three additional business days.

If you see a pending entry matching your flight, no action is needed. If there is no entry at all, compare the SkyMiles number on your booking confirmation against the number on your account profile. A mismatch means the system directed the credit to a different account or rejected it entirely.

You can view your profile number by going to "My Account" → "Profile" on delta.com. If the booking used a wrong or outdated SkyMiles number, contact Delta immediately — the sooner a correction is made, the simpler the recovery process. Old credits directed to mismatched accounts can be retrieved, but they require manual intervention from the SkyMiles team.

It is also worth checking your SkyMiles account activity on the Fly Delta mobile app in addition to the desktop website, as some pending credits display differently across platforms. The app's activity view sometimes shows a "processing" status for recently completed flights that the desktop view does not yet reflect. If neither the app nor the website shows any entry for a flight that you know was operated more than five business days ago, that is a reliable indicator that the mileage record was not received — at that point, a retroactive claim is the appropriate next step rather than continuing to wait.

When to Call Delta About Missing SkyMiles

If the online request system cannot locate your flight, returns an error, or if five business days have passed without a response after submitting your claim, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the most direct path to resolution. A SkyMiles specialist has simultaneous access to your reservation history, flight completion records, and your mileage account — they can see in real time whether a credit was issued and where it went.

Before calling, gather the following: your SkyMiles number, the flight number, travel date, departure and arrival cities, and the original booking confirmation number. For partner airline flights, also have the partner carrier's confirmation number. The more information you provide at the start of the call, the faster the agent can resolve the issue.

For credits tied to bonus promotions — such as a limited-time offer for flying a specific route or reaching a mileage threshold — the SkyMiles promotions team handles the posting separately from regular flight miles. Agents at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can submit an internal referral to that team and provide an estimated resolution timeline, which is typically 10 business days after escalation.

Before your call, pull up your original booking confirmation email and have the airline's ticket number on hand — for Delta-operated flights this is a 13-digit number starting with 006, and for partner airline flights it is the partner's ticket number beginning with their own airline prefix. Agents can search by ticket number even when the SkyMiles number was not attached to the original booking, which significantly speeds up the process compared to a name and date search. Having this number ready at the start of the call typically shortens resolution time by 10 to 15 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for SkyMiles to post after a Delta flight?

SkyMiles typically post within 72 hours of a completed Delta flight. If they have not appeared within five business days, check your account activity tab for pending entries, then file a missing miles request online or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 for faster resolution.

Can I add my SkyMiles number to a booking after I have already flown?

Yes — Delta accepts retroactive mileage claims for flights within the past six months. Submit the request through your SkyMiles account online or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 with your flight confirmation number and travel date. Partner airline flights may have a shorter retroactive window set by the operating carrier.

What if my SkyMiles number was entered incorrectly on my booking?

A wrong SkyMiles number directs the credit to a different account or causes it to fail entirely. Contact Delta as soon as possible by calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181. Agents can search by passenger name and flight to locate the intended credit and transfer it to your correct account once your identity is verified.

Why are my partner airline SkyMiles taking so long to post?

Partner airline mileage credits — for flights on Air France, KLM, Virgin Atlantic, and other SkyTeam carriers — can take four to six weeks, as reporting runs between two separate airlines. If it has been longer than six weeks, file a claim through the operating airline's frequent flyer portal or contact Delta for assistance.

Do Delta SkyMiles expire?

Delta SkyMiles do not expire as long as your account remains active. Activity is defined as any mileage earn or redemption on the account. If your account has been inactive for an extended period, sign in to verify the status of your miles before they are affected by any account maintenance policy.

Can I claim miles for a Delta flight I took more than six months ago?

Delta's standard retroactive window is six months. Claims for older flights require manual review and are not guaranteed. If there were documented technical issues with the posting, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to discuss whether an exception review is possible.

Will I earn SkyMiles on a Basic Economy ticket?

Yes — Basic Economy fares earn SkyMiles, though the mileage earning rate is lower than Main Cabin or premium fares. The exact amount depends on the fare paid and your Medallion status. If miles from a Basic Economy flight did not post within five business days, file a retroactive claim online or contact Delta directly.

How do I earn bonus SkyMiles from Delta promotions?

Delta runs promotional bonus mileage offers tied to specific routes, time windows, or activities. Promotional miles follow a separate posting schedule — often six to eight weeks after the qualifying flight. Check your SkyMiles account activity for a pending promotional entry before assuming the credit is missing.

Can I earn SkyMiles on flights booked through a travel agent?

Yes — miles post based on the SkyMiles number attached to the ticket, regardless of how the ticket was purchased. Ask your travel agent to add your SkyMiles number at the time of booking. If it was omitted, add it through 'Manage My Trip' on delta.com before the flight, or submit a retroactive claim afterward.

What is the difference between base miles and bonus miles on a Delta flight?

Base miles are calculated from the distance flown and the fare class purchased. Bonus miles are added on top based on your Medallion status, the cabin, or an active promotion. Both appear in your activity tab within a few days of your flight. If either type is missing beyond five business days, file a mileage request to have the discrepancy reviewed.
