How to Use SkyMiles for a Delta Seat Upgrade

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to have a Delta specialist check real-time upgrade availability on your specific flight and process the upgrade with your SkyMiles during the call. Delta offers award upgrades from Main Cabin to Comfort+, First Class, and — on long-haul routes — Delta One business class. The number of SkyMiles required varies by route distance, cabin, and how far in advance you request the upgrade. Knowing the right timing and method can make the difference between a confirmed seat in the front cabin and landing on a long waitlist.

Types of Delta Upgrades Available with SkyMiles

Delta offers two primary ways to use SkyMiles for cabin upgrades: Pay with Miles upgrades and complimentary Medallion upgrades — and they work very differently.

Pay with Miles: This option lets any SkyMiles member purchase an upgrade to Comfort+ or First Class using a combination of cash and SkyMiles. The cost varies by route and is displayed at booking on delta.com. Pay with Miles upgrades are subject to availability and are confirmed immediately upon purchase, making them more predictable than waitlist-based upgrades.

Award Upgrades: Available for Medallion members and occasionally for general members during promotional periods, these use SkyMiles to request an upgrade on a fully ticketed Main Cabin reservation. The mileage cost for an award upgrade depends on the route — domestic short-haul upgrades cost fewer miles than transcontinental or international routes. Award upgrade availability opens at different times depending on your Medallion tier: Diamond members get the earliest access, followed by Platinum, Gold, and Silver.

For the current SkyMiles cost of an upgrade on your specific flight, check the "Upgrade Options" section in Manage My Trip on delta.com, or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to have an agent pull real-time pricing for your route.

It is worth distinguishing between the "Pay with Miles" feature and a full award upgrade. Pay with Miles lets you apply SkyMiles to reduce the cash price of an upgraded cabin — you pay a mix of miles and dollars rather than the full cash price. A full award upgrade, by contrast, uses a fixed mileage amount to move from a paid Main Cabin ticket into an available premium cabin seat. Pay with Miles is flexible and always shows a price as long as the upgraded cabin has any availability; full award upgrades require the specific "upgrade award" inventory to be open, which is often more restricted. Understanding which option you are selecting at checkout prevents surprises about the mileage cost.

How Upgrade Waitlists Work on Delta

When confirmed upgrades are unavailable, Delta places eligible passengers on a waitlist ordered by Medallion tier and the number of SkyMiles committed. Diamond Medallion members receive the highest priority, followed by Platinum, Gold, Silver, and then general SkyMiles members.

Within each Medallion tier, waitlist position is further determined by the type of upgrade instrument being used and, in some cases, the fare class of the original ticket — higher fare classes within the same Medallion tier receive earlier consideration. On routes that are sold out in premium cabin, waitlists can be long even for Diamond members.

Waitlist positions are updated continuously until the gate is closed. If upgrade space opens — due to a cancellation, a premium cabin passenger missing the flight, or Delta adjusting seat inventory — the system automatically processes upgrades in waitlist order. You receive an email and a push notification through the Fly Delta app when your upgrade clears.

To check your waitlist position before the day of travel, open the Fly Delta app and navigate to your trip. The upgrade waitlist section shows your current position and the number of passengers ahead of you. If you have specific questions about a waitlist, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 lets an agent pull the live status for your flight.

For Medallion members, Delta's system automatically adds you to upgrade waitlists for eligible flights when you check in, provided you have the appropriate certificates or miles in your account. You do not need to manually join the waitlist in most cases — the check-in process does this automatically for flights where your status tier makes you eligible. If you want to proactively attach an upgrade certificate before the 24-hour check-in window opens, go to "Manage My Trip" and look for the upgrade option. Attaching the certificate early locks in your waitlist position earlier, which can be meaningful on routes where the waitlist gets long quickly.

Best Timing to Request a Delta Upgrade

Timing is a significant factor in upgrade success. Delta's upgrade inventory — particularly for Medallion-based complimentary upgrades — tends to open wider in two windows: at the time of booking (when the first seats are made available) and within 24–72 hours of departure (when Delta releases unsold premium inventory).

For Pay with Miles upgrades, checking availability immediately after booking and again 24 hours before departure gives you the best chance. If the upgrade is not shown in Manage My Trip, the cabin may be sold out or restricted — but availability changes frequently as other passengers modify their bookings.

For Medallion upgrade certificates, Delta Platinum and Diamond members can request upgrades at booking. Gold and Silver members typically join the waitlist closer to departure. The exact availability window for your status level is shown in your SkyMiles account under "Upgrade Preferences."

If the day before departure arrives and you are still on the waitlist, checking in exactly at the 24-hour mark through the Fly Delta app ensures you are confirmed on the waitlist with the best available position heading into the gate phase. Agents at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can also assist with last-minute upgrade inquiries on the day of travel.

Checking in at exactly the 24-hour mark through the Fly Delta app has two benefits for upgrade purposes: it confirms your waitlist position ahead of passengers who check in later, and it triggers the app to display your current waitlist rank with a live counter of how many passengers are ahead of you. If you are within five spots of the top of the list and the flight has premium seats still showing as occupied, there is a reasonable chance one or more will open up as other passengers cancel or take earlier connections. Monitoring the app in the hours before departure gives you a real-time sense of your odds without having to call.

Using SkyMiles for Delta One or International Upgrades

Upgrading to Delta One — Delta's long-haul business class cabin — using SkyMiles requires a higher mileage commitment than domestic upgrades. The exact number of miles depends on the origin and destination, the cabin you are upgrading from, and the availability category of the space.

International award upgrades are available on select Delta-operated routes and on certain partner airline flights within Delta's program. Partner upgrades have separate mileage requirements and availability, and not all partners participate in the upgrade program at all.

Delta One upgrade space tends to be tightly controlled, particularly on high-demand transatlantic and transpacific routes. The most reliable way to confirm whether award upgrade space is open on a specific international flight is to check the "Upgrade" tab in Manage My Trip or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and ask an agent to query the upgrade inventory by flight number and date. Agents can also explain the exact mileage cost, any additional fees, and how the upgrade interacts with your existing seat assignment or special requests.

If you are targeting a specific international flight for a Delta One upgrade and the award space is not currently showing as available, it is worth checking back every few days rather than concluding the opportunity is gone. Delta's revenue management team adjusts award space availability throughout the booking window, and space that was closed six weeks out sometimes opens two to three weeks before departure as the airline optimizes seat allocation between paid premium passengers and award redemptions. Setting a reminder to check the upgrade tab in Manage My Trip weekly — and asking an agent to note a standing interest on your file — can help you capture a space the moment it opens.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many SkyMiles does it cost to upgrade a Delta flight?

The cost varies by route, cabin, and availability. A domestic Comfort+ upgrade may cost a few thousand SkyMiles, while a Delta One upgrade on a long international route can require tens of thousands. Check the upgrade pricing in Manage My Trip on delta.com, or call Delta's customer service team for the exact mileage cost on your specific flight.

Can I upgrade a Basic Economy Delta ticket with SkyMiles?

Basic Economy tickets are not eligible for upgrades, regardless of Medallion status or SkyMiles available. To be eligible for a cabin upgrade, you need a Main Cabin or higher fare. If your plans allow flexibility, rebooking into a Main Cabin fare opens upgrade eligibility.

When does upgrade availability open for Medallion members?

Upgrade availability opens earlier for higher Medallion tiers. Diamond members can request upgrades at booking, while Platinum members receive access before departure. Gold and Silver members typically join the waitlist closer to the flight date. Exact windows depend on the route and seat availability.

Can I upgrade using SkyMiles if I am not a Medallion member?

Yes — Delta's Pay with Miles option allows any SkyMiles member to purchase a Comfort+ or First Class upgrade using miles and cash, subject to availability. Award upgrade certificates are typically a Medallion benefit, but general members can use Pay with Miles when the option is displayed in Manage My Trip. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 for current availability on your flight.

What happens to my upgrade if my flight is canceled or changed?

If Delta cancels your flight and rebooks you on a different one, the upgraded seat may not carry over automatically. Contact Delta as soon as the rebooking happens to request that the upgrade be applied to the new flight, subject to availability. SkyMiles used for the original upgrade are typically redeposited if the seat cannot be transferred.

Can I use a Delta upgrade certificate instead of SkyMiles?

Platinum and Diamond Medallion members receive Complimentary Upgrade Certificates annually. These certificates are applied to the upgrade waitlist rather than guaranteeing a seat. If you have a certificate and are unsure how to attach it to a specific booking, delta.com or the Fly Delta app shows how to apply it in the manage trip flow.

Does upgrading with SkyMiles earn additional miles on the new cabin?

When you upgrade using Pay with Miles or an award upgrade, your mileage earning is typically based on the original purchased fare class, not the upgraded cabin. You do not earn miles as if you paid for the premium cabin outright unless the upgrade is a paid fare difference.

Can I upgrade a companion ticket with SkyMiles?

Award upgrades and Pay with Miles upgrades can sometimes be applied to companion tickets on the same reservation, but availability and eligibility depend on how the companion ticket was booked. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to confirm whether an upgrade can be applied to your specific companion booking and what the cost would be.

What if I am bumped from a First Class upgrade I already paid for?

If you paid for an upgrade and Delta moves you back to a lower cabin due to an aircraft change or oversale, you are entitled to a refund of the upgrade cost — either in SkyMiles or cash, depending on how you paid. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 if this occurs and an agent can process the refund and document the situation on your account.

Is it better to upgrade at booking or wait until closer to the flight?

Upgrading at booking locks in the seat immediately if space is available but at the posted price. Waiting until closer to departure — particularly 24 to 48 hours before — sometimes reveals lower upgrade prices as Delta releases remaining inventory. However, there is no guarantee space will be available later, so confirmed immediate upgrades provide more certainty.
