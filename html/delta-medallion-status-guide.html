The Delta Medallion program is a four-tier elite program that rewards frequent flyers with progressively more valuable benefits as their annual spending on Delta flights and eligible purchases increases. This guide covers everything from how to qualify for the first tier to what Diamond Medallion status actually delivers.

The Four Medallion Tiers and How to Qualify

Delta Medallion status is earned through Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs) — a spending-based metric that replaced the previous combination of miles-flown and dollar thresholds in 2023. MQDs are earned through qualifying Delta flight spending and, up to an annual cap, through eligible Delta American Express card purchases.

Silver Medallion: Requires $5,000 MQDs annually. Entry-level elite status that delivers priority boarding in Zone 5, a 5x SkyMiles earn rate on Delta flights, complimentary seat selection in the Main Cabin, and discounted Sky Club day pass rates. Silver is the threshold that converts occasional Delta travelers into recognized elite members.

Gold Medallion: Requires $10,000 MQDs annually. Upgrades the boarding to Zone 3, increases the SkyMiles earn rate to 8x on Delta flights, and adds complimentary first checked item, priority check-in and security where available, and upgrade waitlist access to Comfort+ on domestic flights.

Platinum Medallion: Requires $15,000 MQDs annually. Moves to Zone 2 boarding, raises the SkyMiles earn rate to 9x, provides complimentary Sky Club access on same-day Delta flights, expands upgrade eligibility to include domestic First Class, and adds Global Entry or TSA PreCheck fee reimbursement and four systemwide upgrade certificates.

Diamond Medallion: Requires $35,000 MQDs annually. The top tier provides Zone 1 boarding, a 12x SkyMiles earn rate, unlimited complimentary Sky Club access with two guests, upgrade priority at the highest level, Delta One upgrades on select international routes, and a dedicated Delta 360 service team. Diamond members also receive complimentary companion upgrades and additional systemwide certificates. Benefits That Matter Most at Each Medallion Level

Understanding which Medallion benefits deliver the most real value — not just the ones that look impressive on paper — helps you evaluate whether pursuing a higher tier is worth the additional spend.

At Silver level, the most practical benefits are the 5x mileage earn rate and priority boarding. The boarding priority alone is tangible on full flights, allowing you to board before overhead bin space is full. The discounted Sky Club rate is useful if you visit clubs occasionally.

At Gold level, the complimentary first checked item and 8x mileage earn rate are the most regularly used perks. If you check an item regularly on domestic travel, the savings across multiple trips can exceed the annual cost of reaching Gold through ticket purchases alone.

At Platinum level, complimentary Sky Club access transforms the airport experience — particularly on long layovers or early morning departures. The four annual systemwide upgrade certificates provide confirmed premium cabin seats, which is a meaningful benefit for specific high-value trips. The Global Entry reimbursement alone is worth $100.

At Diamond level, the unlimited Sky Club access with two guests and the Delta One upgrade eligibility on international routes represent the program's peak benefits. Diamond members also receive exclusive phone lines with shorter hold times and dedicated agents. If you travel internationally multiple times per year, Diamond-level benefits can be worth thousands of dollars annually in premium cabin access. Earning MQDs Through Flights and Delta Amex Cards

MQDs are the single metric that determines your Medallion tier, and there are two ways to accumulate them: through Delta flight spending and through eligible Delta American Express card spending.

Flight MQDs: Qualifying Delta-operated flights earn MQDs based on the fare paid — not the distance flown. A $500 Main Cabin ticket earns $500 in MQDs. A $2,000 First Class fare earns $2,000 in MQDs. Only the base ticket price generates MQDs — taxes, fees, and separately purchased upgrades do not count. Partner airline flights, even when booked through Delta, do not earn MQDs.

Card MQDs: Delta SkyMiles American Express cards allow cardholders to earn MQDs through everyday spending, up to an annual cap that varies by card tier. The Delta Reserve Amex provides the highest annual MQD cap from card spending. These card MQDs count directly toward your Medallion qualification, making the Reserve card particularly valuable for travelers who are close to a tier threshold at year end.

MQDs earned during the calendar year (January 1 through December 31) determine your status for the following calendar year. Status earned in 2025 is active from January 2026 through January 31, 2027, giving you an additional month in the new year before the status period formally ends. To check your current MQD progress, sign in to delta.com — the account dashboard shows your running total and how far you are from the next tier. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 if you believe MQDs from a recent flight or card statement have not posted correctly.

What Happens at Year-End and How to Protect Your Status

The Medallion qualification year closes on December 31. Delta calculates your final MQD total and assigns your status tier for the upcoming year. If you have not reached your current tier's threshold by year end, Delta automatically assigns you to the tier your actual MQD total qualifies for — or removes elite status entirely if you fall below Silver.

Your existing status remains active through January 31 of the new year, providing a brief window to use remaining Medallion benefits — including Sky Club access, upgrade certificates, and priority services — before the new status takes effect in February.

To protect or requalify for your current tier as December approaches, consider: booking any remaining Delta travel before December 31 (departures must occur, not just be booked, before the year closes), maximizing spending on your Delta Amex card to reach the card MQD cap for the year, and reviewing your current MQD balance weekly in the final months to avoid surprises.

Delta does not offer consistent public status extension programs, but calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 before the year ends to discuss your situation — particularly if you experienced a Delta-caused disruption that prevented planned travel — opens the possibility of a case-by-case review by the Medallion service team. Acting before December 31 provides more options than acting after.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs)?

MQDs are the spending-based metric Delta uses to determine Medallion tier. You earn $1 MQD for every $1 spent on qualifying Delta-operated flights, and additional MQDs through eligible Delta Amex card spending up to an annual cap. MQDs reset to zero each January 1. For your current MQD balance and tier progress, sign in to delta.com or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181.

What is the cheapest way to reach Silver Medallion on Delta?

Silver Medallion requires $5,000 in MQDs annually. The most efficient path combines Delta flight spending with Delta Amex card spending to reach the threshold. A Delta Reserve Amex card contributes the highest annual MQD cap from card spending, reducing the amount of flight spending needed to qualify.

Do Delta Medallion benefits apply on partner airline flights?

Some Medallion benefits extend to partner airline flights — for example, priority boarding and waived fees on SkyTeam carriers. However, complimentary upgrade eligibility and Sky Club access generally apply only on Delta-operated flights. Check the specific benefit terms for your Medallion tier on delta.com.

When does my Delta Medallion status start after qualifying?

Medallion status earned during the qualification year (January 1–December 31) takes effect at the start of the following calendar year. If you qualify for Gold during 2025, your Gold status is active from January 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027. The extra month at the start of the next year reflects the period before the new year's qualification begins to register.

Can I get Delta Medallion status through a status match?

Delta occasionally offers status match or challenge programs for elite members of competing airlines, though availability is not consistently public. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to ask whether a status match is currently available for your account. You will typically need to provide documentation of your status with the other airline.

How do I check my Medallion status progress?

Sign in to delta.com and navigate to your SkyMiles account. The dashboard shows your current MQD balance, your tier progress bar toward the next level, and how many MQDs remain until the next threshold. Medallion members can also call the Medallion line on the back of their card for a real-time progress update.

What is Delta Diamond Medallion status?

Diamond Medallion is Delta's top elite tier, requiring $35,000 MQDs annually. Benefits include unlimited Sky Club access with two guests, Zone 1 boarding, a 12x SkyMiles earn rate, systemwide upgrade certificates, Delta One upgrade eligibility on select routes, and dedicated Medallion service. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to discuss Diamond-level benefits for your specific travel patterns.

Does Medallion status give me free upgrades on every Delta flight?

Medallion status provides upgrade priority and eligibility, but upgrades are not guaranteed on every flight. Availability depends on unsold premium cabin seats and the number of other eligible Medallion members requesting upgrades on the same flight. Diamond and Platinum Medallion members receive earlier access to upgrade windows, increasing the likelihood of clearing before departure.

Can my family members use my Medallion benefits?

Most Medallion benefits are tied to the account holder's travel. However, Sky Club guest access (for Diamond and Platinum members traveling with guests) and the annual companion certificate (for Reserve Amex cardholders) extend select benefits to accompanying travelers. Systemwide upgrade certificates can typically be applied to a companion on the same itinerary.

What happens to my upgrade certificates if I lose Medallion status?

Unused systemwide upgrade certificates issued as part of a Medallion year's benefits typically expire when that status period ends — January 31 of the year following your qualification year. Use any active certificates before losing status, as they generally cannot be extended or carried into a lower tier.
