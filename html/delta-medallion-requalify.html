How to Requalify for Delta Medallion Status

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to speak with a Delta Medallion specialist who can review your qualification progress and explain exactly what you need to requalify before the year-end deadline. Delta's Medallion program rewards frequent flyers with four tiers — Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond — each requiring a combination of Medallion Qualification Dollars spent on Delta flights and eligible purchases. Understanding the specific thresholds and any waiver options available to you can make the difference between maintaining status and starting from scratch.

Delta's Medallion Qualification Requirements

Delta switched to a spend-based qualification model in 2023. Medallion status is now earned primarily through Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs), which are calculated based on the amount you spend on Delta flights and eligible Delta American Express card purchases — not solely on the miles flown.

The annual MQD thresholds for each Medallion tier are as follows: Silver Medallion requires $5,000 MQDs; Gold Medallion requires $10,000 MQDs; Platinum Medallion requires $15,000 MQDs; Diamond Medallion — the top tier — requires $35,000 MQDs. The qualification year runs from January 1 through December 31, and status earned in one year is active through January 31 of the following year.

Delta SkyMiles American Express card holders can accelerate MQD accumulation through card spending. Eligible Amex card purchases — at a rate of $1 MQD per dollar spent up to the card's annual maximum — supplement what you earn through flight spending. If you are close to a threshold and own an eligible Delta Amex card, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 helps you understand exactly how much card spending is needed to close the gap before December 31.

It is worth noting that MQDs from Amex card spending are posted to your SkyMiles account on the same cycle as your card's statement closing date — they do not post in real time as purchases are made. If your card statement closes on the 15th of each month, any card spending made between the 1st and the 15th will post to your SkyMiles MQD total within a few business days after the 15th. Planning significant card spending to close before December 31 means accounting for this cycle — a large purchase made on December 29th may not post MQDs until after the January statement, which would count those MQDs toward next year's qualification instead of the current one.

Strategies for Requalifying Before Year-End

If you are behind on MQDs as the qualification deadline approaches, there are several strategies to accelerate your progress.

Booking high-yield flights: Premium cabin tickets — First Class, Delta One, and Comfort+ — generate significantly more MQDs per trip than Basic Economy or Main Cabin fares. A single transatlantic business class booking can contribute hundreds of MQDs in one transaction.

Using your Delta Amex card: If you hold a Delta SkyMiles American Express card, every eligible purchase contributes MQDs. The annual MQD earn cap varies by card tier — consult your card agreement or call Amex for your specific cap.

Status matches and challenges: If you hold elite status with another airline, Delta sometimes offers a status match or challenge program that provides a faster route to Medallion status. Availability varies and is not always publicly advertised. Calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the best way to ask whether a match or challenge is currently available for your situation.

Timing early bookings: MQDs are earned based on the date of travel, not the date of purchase. Any flight you plan to take before December 31 must actually depart before midnight on December 31 to count toward the current qualification year.

Revenue tickets earn MQDs based on the amount you actually pay for the ticket — taxes, fees, and upgrades purchased at the time of ticketing are included in the qualifying amount. Award tickets booked with SkyMiles, on the other hand, do not earn MQDs regardless of how much the taxes and fees amount to. If you are approaching a Medallion threshold and are deciding between booking a paid ticket or redeeming an award, the MQD contribution of the paid ticket may be enough to close the gap, making the paid option financially worthwhile even if the mileage redemption looks attractive on paper.

What Happens When You Don't Requalify

If you fall short of your current tier's MQD threshold by December 31, Delta automatically downgrades your Medallion status to the tier your actual MQD total qualifies for — or removes it entirely if you fall below the Silver threshold. Your existing status remains active through January 31 of the following year, giving you a brief window to redeem any remaining Medallion benefits before they expire.

Complimentary upgrades, free checked items, bonus SkyMiles earning, and priority boarding all reflect your current active status — they update to match your new tier once the status change takes effect on February 1.

If you believe there are MQDs missing from your account — perhaps from a recent flight or an Amex card spend that has not yet posted — call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 before the December 31 deadline. Agents can manually review your MQD balance, identify any pending credits, and in some cases expedite the posting of credits that are delayed in processing. Waiting until January to discover a shortfall may be too late to correct.

Ancillary purchases made during a Delta flight — such as Wi-Fi, in-flight entertainment upgrades, or seat upgrades purchased at the gate — do not contribute to MQDs. Only the fare itself and eligible Delta Amex card spending count toward annual qualification. Similarly, eCredits applied toward a new ticket reduce the out-of-pocket cash paid, which in turn affects the MQDs earned on that booking — you earn MQDs on the net amount paid, not the original ticket price. If you regularly book with eCredits, factor this in when estimating how close you are to your MQD target heading into the fourth quarter.

Delta Medallion Status Extensions and Exceptions

Delta has, on occasion, offered status extensions for passengers who faced extraordinary disruptions — such as widespread travel restrictions or documented emergencies — that prevented them from reaching their MQD target. These extensions are handled on a case-by-case basis and are not a standard published policy.

To request a status review or extension, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the appropriate channel. Have your SkyMiles number, current MQD balance, and an explanation of any circumstances that affected your travel ready before the call. Delta's Medallion team handles these requests separately from general customer service and can access your full account history to evaluate the request fairly.

Additionally, if you experienced a Delta-controlled cancellation or significant delay that forced you to miss a planned flight — one that would have contributed meaningfully to your MQD total — that is a specific and documentable situation that the Medallion team can take into account. Keep records of any Delta communications related to canceled or significantly altered flights, as they support your case during a status review.

If you are requesting a status extension due to documented medical or personal circumstances, the most effective approach is to have your supporting materials ready before placing the call — a physician's letter, a hospital discharge summary, or a documented Delta cancellation notice that forced you to miss qualifying travel. The Medallion team's decision on exceptions is partially driven by account history, so longer-tenured Medallion members with strong prior qualification records tend to receive more favorable consideration. If your first request is declined, ask the agent to document the inquiry on your account and note that you may follow up with additional supporting documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs)?

MQDs are the primary currency for earning Delta Medallion status. You earn them by spending on Delta-operated flights and through eligible Delta American Express card purchases. The amount of MQDs earned per flight is based on the fare paid, not the distance flown. For a breakdown of your current MQD balance, sign in to delta.com or call Delta's customer service team.

When does the Delta Medallion qualification year end?

The qualification year runs from January 1 through December 31. Status earned during the year becomes active for the following calendar year and remains valid through January 31 of that year. Any flights taken after December 31 count toward the next qualification year, not the current one.

Can Delta Amex card spending help me requalify for Medallion status?

Yes — eligible Delta SkyMiles American Express card purchases contribute MQDs at a rate set by your specific card tier, up to an annual cap. If you are close to a Medallion threshold, combining card spending with flight spending before December 31 can help close the gap. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to confirm how much additional MQD credit your card activity would add to your current total.

Does Delta offer a Medallion status match from other airlines?

Delta occasionally offers status match or challenge programs for elite members of other airlines, though availability is not consistently publicized. Calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the best way to ask whether a status match is available for your account and what documentation would be required.

What benefits do I keep if my Medallion status is downgraded?

Your current Medallion benefits remain active through January 31 of the year following your qualification year. After that date, benefits update to reflect your new status tier. Complimentary upgrades, priority boarding, bonus miles earning, and waived fees all adjust accordingly on February 1.

Can I requalify for Medallion status using partner airline flights?

Delta Medallion status is earned exclusively through Delta-operated flights and eligible Delta Amex card spending. Flights operated by partner carriers — even when booked through Delta — do not contribute MQDs. Only the Delta flight number (DL-coded) segments count toward Medallion qualification.

What is a Medallion status challenge?

A Medallion challenge is a program where Delta gives you a set number of months to hit a reduced MQD threshold in exchange for a provisional elite status during the challenge period. Not all members are eligible, and challenge programs are not always available. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to ask whether your account qualifies for a current challenge offer.

How do I check my MQD progress toward Medallion requalification?

Sign in to delta.com and navigate to your SkyMiles account summary. Your MQD balance and progress toward each Medallion tier appear in the dashboard. The page also shows how many MQDs remain until the next tier and any projected contributions from upcoming bookings.

Can I transfer MQDs from a previous year toward current requalification?

No — MQDs reset to zero at the start of each qualification year. There is no carryover of MQDs from prior years. However, Delta has offered rollover MQD programs during certain promotional periods. Check the SkyMiles promotions page or contact Delta to see if any current rollover offer applies to your account.

What happens to my systemwide upgrades if I lose Medallion status?

Unused systemwide upgrades issued as a Medallion benefit typically expire when your status changes. If you have active systemwide upgrades and are at risk of losing status, use them before January 31. Certificates that were issued as part of a specific year's Medallion tier do not carry forward if your status tier changes downward.
