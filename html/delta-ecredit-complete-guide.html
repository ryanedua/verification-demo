Delta eCredits are the primary form of compensation when a non-refundable ticket is canceled, and millions of travelers hold them in various states of use. Understanding how eCredits work — where they live, how to apply them, and what happens when they go missing — ensures you do not leave ticket value unused.

What Is a Delta eCredit and When Is It Issued

A Delta eCredit is a certificate representing the value of a canceled non-refundable ticket. It is stored in your SkyMiles account under "My Wallet" and can be applied toward any future Delta flight booked through delta.com or the Fly Delta app. eCredits are not transferable to other airlines or redeemable for cash — they exist as future Delta travel value only.

eCredits are issued in the following situations: when a non-refundable Main Cabin, Comfort+, or certain First Class ticket is canceled before departure; when Delta proactively cancels or significantly changes your flight and you choose a credit over rebooking; when a fare adjustment creates residual value on a ticket; and in some cases as goodwill compensation for service issues.

The face value of an eCredit equals the unused ticket value at the time of cancellation. If you paid $350 for a ticket and used $0 of it, the eCredit is $350. If you used the outbound leg of a round-trip and then canceled the return, the eCredit reflects only the value of the unused return segment. Taxes and fees paid in cash at booking are typically refunded separately to the original payment method and are not included in the eCredit value.

eCredits issued in 2024 and beyond are valid for 12 months from the original ticket purchase date — not the cancellation date. This distinction matters because a ticket purchased in January 2024 but not canceled until July 2024 still has an eCredit that expires in January 2025. If you are unsure of your eCredit's expiration date, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to confirm before making travel plans based on that credit.

How to Find and Apply an eCredit to a Delta Booking

eCredits are accessible in multiple ways, depending on how the original ticket was booked.

Through your SkyMiles account: Sign in to delta.com, go to "My Account," then "My Wallet," and select "eCredits." All active eCredits linked to your SkyMiles account appear here, organized by certificate number, value, and expiration date. Click any credit to see its full details and the original booking it came from.

By ticket number: If the original ticket was not linked to a SkyMiles account — purchased as a guest — you can still retrieve the eCredit by entering the 13-digit ticket number (beginning with 006) on the eCredits page. Guest-issued eCredits are not automatically visible in a wallet but are fully accessible when you have the ticket number.

At checkout: When booking a new flight on delta.com, proceed through the booking flow to the payment page. Select "eCredits / Gift Cards / Certificates" and enter your eCredit certificate number. The value applies automatically toward the total, and any remaining balance is payable by card or additional SkyMiles.

eCredits can only be applied through delta.com or the Fly Delta app — not through third-party sites, travel agencies, or at the airport ticket counter. If you prefer to book by phone and apply an eCredit during the call, agents at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can process the combined payment in one transaction.

What to Do When an eCredit Is Missing or Has Disappeared

Several scenarios cause eCredits to disappear from a wallet or fail to appear at all, and most are resolvable without losing the credit.

Name mismatch: eCredits are tied to the passenger name on the original ticket. If you recently updated your SkyMiles profile name, or if the original ticket used a slightly different name spelling, the eCredit may not match your current account. It still exists in the system under the original name — a Delta agent can retrieve it by ticket number regardless of the name on file.

Different email or account: If the original booking was made under a different email address or an old SkyMiles account, the eCredit is associated with that login. Search your email archives for the original cancellation confirmation — it contains the certificate number needed to apply the credit even without logging into the account it was issued under.

Already applied: An eCredit that was applied to a subsequent booking and then that booking was also canceled may have been reissued as a new eCredit with a different certificate number. Check your wallet for eCredits with recent issue dates that may represent the reissued version.

If none of these explanations account for the missing credit, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 with the original ticket number. Agents have access to the full reservation history and can trace the credit through the system regardless of whether it is visible to you in the online wallet.

Expired eCredits and Other eCredit Issues

An expired eCredit loses its face value and cannot be applied to new bookings through the standard channels. However, Delta does review expiration situations on a case-by-case basis, and some expired credits are honored or converted depending on the circumstances.

If your eCredit expired because you were unable to travel during the validity period — due to a documented medical situation, a significant Delta-caused disruption, or another compelling reason — call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and explain the circumstances. Have the original ticket number, the eCredit certificate number, and any supporting documentation ready. Agents escalate these requests to Delta's customer experience team, which has discretion to extend or convert expired credits.

If the expiration was simply an oversight, the outcome depends on how recently the credit expired and your overall travel history with Delta. While reinstatement is not guaranteed, it is worth requesting, particularly for Medallion members with a long account history.

Additional common eCredit issues and their solutions: an eCredit that covers only part of the new ticket price can be combined with a credit card or SkyMiles at checkout; an eCredit applied to a booking that you then cancel creates a new eCredit for the remaining value; and two eCredits cannot always be combined in the online booking tool — agents at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can apply multiple credits to a single booking during a phone transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Delta eCredit last?

eCredits issued in 2024 and later are valid for 12 months from the original ticket purchase date — not the cancellation date. If the purchase date was in January, the eCredit expires in January regardless of when the cancellation happened. To confirm your specific eCredit's expiration date, log in to delta.com or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181.

Can I use a Delta eCredit for someone else's ticket?

eCredits are non-transferable in the sense that they must be used by the original ticket holder — a booking that includes that same passenger can use the credit. You cannot sign the credit over to a completely different person who was not on the original ticket.

Can I get cash back instead of a Delta eCredit?

Non-refundable tickets convert to eCredits upon cancellation — not cash. A cash refund is available for refundable tickets, for cancellations within the 24-hour window, and when Delta cancels your flight. If your ticket qualified for a cash refund and an eCredit was issued instead, contact Delta to request a correction.

Can I apply a Delta eCredit to an upgrade?

eCredits can be applied toward any Delta ticket purchase, including tickets in premium cabins, but not directly toward a stand-alone upgrade fee. To use an eCredit for an upgraded cabin, book a premium fare and apply the eCredit toward that fare total at checkout.

What if my eCredit doesn't cover the full fare?

Apply the eCredit at checkout and pay the remaining balance with a credit card, debit card, SkyMiles, or a combination. If you have multiple eCredits, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 — agents can apply more than one eCredit to a single booking during a phone reservation, which the online booking tool may not support automatically.

How do I know what my eCredit is worth?

Sign in to delta.com and go to My Account → My Wallet → eCredits. Each certificate shows the face value, issue date, expiration date, and original ticket number it came from. If the eCredit is not in your wallet, use the ticket number from your cancellation confirmation to look it up directly on the eCredits page.

What if my Delta eCredit expired?

Expired eCredits are not automatically reinstated, but Delta reviews expiration requests on a case-by-case basis. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 with the original ticket number, the certificate number, and the reason the credit went unused. Reinstatement is not guaranteed but is available to request, particularly for Medallion members or when there are documented extenuating circumstances.

Can I use a Delta eCredit on the Fly Delta app?

Yes — eCredits can be applied to bookings made through the Fly Delta app. At the payment step, select the eCredit option and enter the certificate number. The process is identical to booking on delta.com from a mobile device.

Does a Delta eCredit earn SkyMiles?

The new ticket you purchase using an eCredit earns SkyMiles the same way any other ticket would — based on the fare paid and your SkyMiles account's earn rate. The fact that an eCredit covered part or all of the payment does not reduce or eliminate SkyMiles earning.

What happens to my eCredit if I cancel the new booking?

If you apply an eCredit to a new booking and then cancel that booking, the value is reissued as a new eCredit. The certificate number changes, and the new eCredit retains the original ticket's expiration date or adopts a new one based on the current policy. Contact Delta to confirm the exact terms when you cancel a booking that was paid with an eCredit.
