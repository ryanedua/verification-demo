Delta Award Ticket Change and Cancellation Rules

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to get real-time assistance with changing or canceling a Delta SkyMiles award ticket — agents can check availability for new dates, process mile redeposits, and apply any applicable fee waivers in a single call. Delta has simplified its award ticket rules significantly since eliminating redeposit fees for most cancellations, but there are still nuances around partner airline awards, close-in booking fees, and changes versus outright cancellations that are worth understanding before you take action.

Canceling a Delta Award Ticket and Getting Miles Back

Delta eliminated the redeposit fee for most SkyMiles award ticket cancellations in 2020, and this policy remains in effect. When you cancel a Delta-operated award ticket before departure, your SkyMiles are returned to your account — there is no per-mile recovery charge for the cancellation itself.

Taxes and fees paid in cash at the time of booking — such as government-imposed airport taxes or security charges — are also refunded to the original payment method when you cancel. For most domestic U.S. award tickets these fees are small, but international award tickets can carry substantial taxes and fees that you will want confirmed before canceling.

To cancel online, sign in to delta.com, go to "My Trips," select the award reservation, and choose "Cancel." The miles redeposit typically appears in your SkyMiles account within 72 hours. Taxes are refunded to your credit card within seven business days. If you prefer to cancel over the phone or have a complex multi-city award itinerary, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the fastest and most reliable option, as agents can confirm the exact mileage and cash refund before you commit to the cancellation.

When you cancel a Delta-operated award ticket online, the system shows you a cancellation summary that confirms the number of miles being redeposited and the cash taxes and fees being refunded before you confirm the action. Review this screen carefully — the miles figure should match the total used at booking, and the cash refund should match the taxes paid at checkout. If either number looks incorrect, do not confirm the cancellation and instead call Delta to cancel with an agent who can verify the figures against your booking record. Accepting an incorrect redeposit amount is difficult to reverse after the fact.

Changing an Existing Delta Award Ticket

Changing the dates or routing on a Delta award ticket is treated differently from canceling — instead of redepositing miles and rebooking, you modify the existing reservation directly. Delta does not charge a change fee for date or routing changes on most award tickets, but the new itinerary must have award availability at the same or a lower mileage cost, or you pay the difference in SkyMiles.

If the new dates cost more miles than the original booking — due to seasonal pricing or higher demand — Delta will charge the additional SkyMiles at the time of the change. If the new dates cost fewer miles, the difference is redeposited to your account.

Online date changes on award tickets are available for simple round-trip itineraries on delta.com under "Manage My Trip." For complex changes — changing the destination, swapping a connecting city, or modifying a partner airline segment — calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the most efficient approach, as the online tool may not present all available options or handle multi-segment changes cleanly.

When deciding between a date change and a cancel-and-rebook on an award ticket, consider whether the new dates require more or fewer SkyMiles than the original booking. If the new dates cost fewer miles, a date change directly modifies the existing reservation and redeposits the difference — a clean outcome. If the new dates cost significantly more miles and you want to preserve your original mileage outlay, canceling and rebooking gives you complete control over what you commit, but it also means the original award space is released and may be taken by another traveler before you complete the rebook. On high-demand routes, modifying the existing reservation is generally safer than a cancel-rebook sequence.

Partner Airline Award Tickets: Different Rules

Awards on Delta's partner carriers — such as Air France, KLM, Korean Air, and other SkyTeam members — are subject to more restrictive change and cancellation rules than Delta-operated awards. Several key differences apply:

Redeposit fees: Partner airline award cancellations may still incur a redeposit fee, even though Delta-operated award cancellations are fee-free. The exact fee depends on when the cancellation is made relative to departure and the specific partner carrier involved.

Change limitations: Changes to partner airline award tickets are more restricted and may not be possible online at all. Some partner carriers do not permit changes once the award is ticketed, requiring a cancel-and-rebook rather than a direct modification.

Advance cancellation requirement: Some partners require cancellation at least 24–72 hours before departure to qualify for any mileage recovery. Cancellations made closer to departure may result in mileage forfeiture. Before modifying or canceling a partner airline award ticket, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to confirm the exact rules and any fees that apply to your specific reservation before taking action.

When calling about a partner airline award, have the partner carrier's confirmation number in addition to your SkyMiles award booking number. Partner award tickets generate two separate booking records — one in Delta's system and one in the operating carrier's reservation system — and mismatches between these records are a common source of confusion when modifying or canceling. Providing both numbers allows the Delta agent to verify that both records reflect the same passenger information and award details before initiating any change, which prevents the situation where a modification is reflected in one system but not the other, causing check-in issues at the partner carrier's airport.

No-Shows and Last-Minute Cancellations on Award Tickets

Failing to cancel an award ticket before departure — a no-show — results in the miles being forfeited for the unused segment. Unlike paid tickets, which may retain some value in certain situations, award tickets that are not canceled in advance have no recovery path for the SkyMiles after the flight departs.

If you realize you will miss a flight and still have time before departure, cancel the award ticket immediately — even an hour before departure is better than a no-show. Delta's system accepts cancellations up until the scheduled departure time, and the miles redeposit minus any applicable partner fees.

For situations where a last-minute cancellation was impossible due to a genuine emergency, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 after the fact gives you the opportunity to explain the circumstances. While mileage recovery after a no-show is not a published policy, Delta's customer experience team has discretion to review individual cases — particularly for Diamond and Platinum Medallion members with a strong travel history. Documentation of the emergency supports the request.

If the circumstances preventing you from canceling before departure are covered by Delta's published irregular operations policies — for example, if a Delta-operated delay caused you to miss the departure of the award segment — Delta's policy shifts the responsibility for the no-show onto the airline rather than the passenger. In those cases, the miles should be restored without the usual no-show forfeiture, and you may also be eligible for a fee waiver on any applicable redeposit. Keep the Delta communications showing the delay timeline as documentation, since the award ticket system does not always automatically flag a controllable delay as the cause of the missed departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to cancel a Delta SkyMiles award ticket?

Delta eliminated redeposit fees for most award ticket cancellations made before departure. Your SkyMiles return to your account, and taxes paid in cash are refunded to the original payment method. Partner airline award tickets may still carry a redeposit fee depending on the carrier and timing. Call Delta's customer service team to confirm the exact terms for your specific reservation.

How long does it take for miles to redeposit after canceling a Delta award?

SkyMiles from a canceled award ticket typically redeposit within 72 hours. Tax refunds to a credit card take approximately seven business days. If either has not appeared after those windows, contact Delta to confirm the cancellation was processed and request status on the pending credit.

Can I change the dates on a Delta award ticket without a fee?

Yes — Delta does not charge a change fee for date modifications on most award tickets. If the new dates have higher award pricing, you pay the SkyMiles difference. If they cost fewer miles, the difference is redeposited. For changes to partner airline segments, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 first to confirm whether a change is possible and what the cost will be.

What happens if I miss my Delta award flight?

Missing an award flight without canceling first results in forfeiture of the SkyMiles for the uncompleted segments. Cancel as early as possible — even minutes before departure — to avoid this. If an emergency prevented cancellation, contact Delta after the fact to request a mileage recovery review on a case-by-case basis.

Can I upgrade a Delta award ticket to a higher cabin?

Upgrades from an economy award to a premium cabin award depend on award availability in the higher cabin. If premium award space is open, you can modify the ticket to the higher cabin and pay the SkyMiles difference. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to check whether the specific cabin upgrade you want is available on your flight date.

Are there close-in booking fees for Delta award tickets?

Delta eliminated close-in booking fees for most award tickets booked directly through SkyMiles. Awards booked within 21 days of departure do not carry a surcharge. However, partner airline awards accessed through Delta may have their own close-in fees — confirm with a Delta agent when booking close to departure.

Can I book a one-way award ticket on Delta?

Yes — Delta allows one-way award ticket bookings. The mileage cost for a one-way award is approximately half the round-trip cost on most domestic routes. International one-way awards are also available, though pricing varies more by route and cabin than domestic awards.

What if the award I want is unavailable on my preferred date?

Award availability changes frequently as other travelers modify or cancel bookings. Check multiple dates within your travel window and try flexible date searches on delta.com. If you are targeting a specific date that is currently unavailable, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to ask about waitlist options or alternative routing that may reach your destination with available award space.

Can I use SkyMiles from a family member's account for my award ticket?

SkyMiles must be redeemed by the account holder. Delta does not allow you to pool miles from multiple accounts into a single reservation. However, the miles in your account can be used to book tickets for family members — the account holder must call or book online, but the ticket can be in another person's name.

Do award tickets earn SkyMiles on the flights?

Award tickets earned through SkyMiles do not earn additional SkyMiles on the flights flown. You fly for free using your existing miles, but the journey itself is not a mileage-earning trip. If earning additional miles is important, consider purchasing a discounted paid ticket rather than redeeming an award for that route.
