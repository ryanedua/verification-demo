What to Do When You Miss a Delta Connection

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 immediately when you realize you will miss or have already missed a Delta connection — a live agent can rebook you on the next available flight and document the disruption in your record while you are still at the airport. Whether the missed connection was caused by a delayed inbound Delta flight, a long security line, or an extended gate-to-gate transit, the steps you take in the first 30 minutes determine how quickly you get to your destination and what compensation you may be entitled to receive.

Delta's Missed Connection Policy: What You Need to Know

Delta distinguishes between two types of missed connections, and the distinction determines what the airline owes you.

Controllable missed connections occur when a Delta-operated delay — a late inbound aircraft, a crew scheduling issue, a mechanical problem, or a gate change — causes you to miss your connecting flight. In these situations, Delta is obligated to rebook you on the next available Delta flight at no charge and, depending on the length of the delay, may provide meal vouchers, hotel accommodation, and transportation to and from the hotel.

Non-controllable missed connections include disruptions caused by severe weather, air traffic control holds, or circumstances outside Delta's direct operation. In these cases, Delta will still rebook you, but the obligation to provide additional compensation is more limited. Delta's Customer Commitment document, available on delta.com, outlines the specific provisions for each scenario.

Regardless of the cause, if you booked a connecting itinerary through Delta — meaning both legs are on the same reservation — Delta is responsible for getting you to your final destination, even if it requires rerouting you through a different hub or placing you on a partner airline. Separate bookings where you connected yourself between two independent tickets do not carry this protection.

When gathering documentation for a missed connection caused by a Delta delay, take a timestamped screenshot of the departure board showing the inbound flight's delay alongside the scheduled departure time of your connecting flight. This single image establishes the causal link between the delay and the missed connection — information that becomes important if you later submit an out-of-pocket expense reimbursement request or a formal complaint. Delta's gate agents are trained to handle irregular operations efficiently, but having your own documentation ensures you have an accurate record independent of what any agent enters in the system at the time.

Immediate Steps When You Miss a Delta Connection

The moment you know you will miss a connection, take action on two fronts simultaneously.

Head to the nearest Delta gate agent or Delta Sky Club: Gate agents at the connecting airport can rebook you in person and print a new boarding pass immediately. If the airport has a Delta Sky Club and you have access, the agents there often have shorter queues and the same rebooking capabilities as gate staff.

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 at the same time: Phone agents often have faster access to inventory than gate staff during high-disruption periods when all agents are managing multiple passengers simultaneously. Using both channels at the same time — keeping you at the gate while a family member or travel companion calls — can speed up the process significantly. Whoever resolves the rebooking first wins.

When you reach an agent — in person or by phone — have your booking confirmation number, your destination, and the flight number of the connection you missed ready. State clearly that you need to reach your final destination as soon as possible. If the next Delta flight is not until the following day, ask about partner airline options and hotel accommodation.

Major Delta hub airports — particularly Atlanta's Hartsfield-Jackson (ATL), Minneapolis-Saint Paul (MSP), Salt Lake City (SLC), and Seattle-Tacoma (SEA) — have dedicated Delta service centers near connecting gates that are separate from the main ticket counter. These service center agents specialize in irregular operations and rebooking during high-disruption periods, which means shorter wait times than the main counter and agents who are more familiar with the full range of rebooking tools available. If you land in Atlanta or another Delta hub after missing a connection, look for the Delta Service Center signage rather than joining the general ticket counter line.

Need help right now? Call Delta support at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 — specialists available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Rebooking Options and What Delta Will Offer

When Delta rebooks you after a controllable delay, the standard options are: rebooking on the next available Delta direct flight to your destination, or rebooking through a different connecting hub if a direct option is not available until much later. Delta agents have the ability to rebook you on any carrier if no reasonable Delta option exists within a specified time frame — ask specifically about this if the next Delta flight is more than six hours away.

For overnight delays caused by controllable disruptions, Delta typically provides:

  • Meal vouchers for use at airport restaurants
  • Hotel accommodation at a contracted partner hotel near the airport
  • Ground transportation vouchers to and from the hotel

These provisions are not automatically handed out — you need to ask the gate agent or call center agent explicitly. If an agent tells you that a hotel is not available, ask to escalate to a supervisor and note the name of the agent you spoke with. Delta's Customer Commitment is a public document and it specifies the provisions for controllable overnight delays.

If Delta's automated rebooking puts you on a connection through a different hub that significantly extends your travel time, you have the right to ask the rebooking agent for alternative routings — including non-stop options to your destination that may have a higher fare class, or options through a hub you prefer. Agents typically present the first available option, not necessarily the best one for your preferences. Being specific about constraints — arriving before a certain time, avoiding a particular hub due to past misconnection experience — helps the agent search more tailored alternatives than what the system generates automatically.

Documenting the Disruption for Future Follow-Up

Keep records of everything related to the disruption: take a photo of the departure board showing the delay, save all emails and text notifications Delta sends, retain any vouchers or compensation offers made at the airport, and note the names of any agents who assist you.

If you incurred out-of-pocket costs during the disruption — a meal not covered by a voucher, a hotel room booked independently because Delta's contracted hotel was full — document those expenses with receipts. Delta's customer care team reviews reimbursement requests for out-of-pocket costs caused by controllable delays, and having itemized receipts substantially improves the outcome.

After you have reached your destination, if you feel the compensation offered during the disruption was inadequate or if expenses you submitted were rejected, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 opens a formal customer care review. Provide the disruption date, your original booking confirmation number, the flight numbers involved, and a description of what occurred. Delta's care team typically responds within seven business days.

After you have arrived at your final destination, Delta's feedback form at delta.com is the appropriate channel for formal complaints about how the disruption was handled — not social media or third-party review sites, which do not generate a case number and are not reviewed by Delta's customer care team. Use the "Comment/Complaint" form, select "Flight Irregularity" as the category, and include your original booking confirmation number, the affected flight numbers and dates, and a clear description of what occurred and what resolution you are seeking. A case number is generated immediately and allows you to track the response timeline through Delta's online portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Delta owe me if I miss a connection due to their delay?

If a Delta-operated delay caused you to miss a connection, Delta is obligated to rebook you on the next available flight at no charge. For overnight delays due to controllable causes, Delta typically provides meal vouchers, hotel accommodation, and transportation. For specifics on your situation, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 while still at the airport.

Does Delta rebook me automatically when I miss a connection?

Delta's system often initiates an automatic rebooking when it detects a connection cannot be made due to an inbound delay. Check the Fly Delta app for an updated itinerary. If no rebooking appears, go to the nearest Delta gate agent or call the airline directly for manual rebooking assistance.

Can I get on an earlier flight if I miss my original connection?

Yes — ask the rebooking agent to place you on the earliest available Delta flight to your destination, which may be through a different hub. Agents have tools to see all available options across Delta's network in real time. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 if you cannot reach a gate agent quickly enough.

What if my next Delta flight after a missed connection is the next day?

For overnight rebooking due to a controllable Delta delay, request hotel accommodation, meal vouchers, and transportation at the airport. These are not automatically provided — you need to ask a gate agent or call Delta directly. If the airport hotel is unavailable through Delta, keep your receipt from a self-booked hotel and submit a reimbursement request afterward.

Does Delta cover my missed connection if I booked two separate tickets?

No — Delta's responsibility for missed connections applies only to itineraries booked as a single reservation. If you self-connected between two separate bookings, Delta treats each ticket independently and is not obligated to rebook you due to a delay on the first ticket.

Can I use my Delta Medallion status to get priority rebooking after a missed connection?

Yes — Diamond and Platinum Medallion members typically receive priority consideration in rebooking queues during irregular operations. Higher-tier members may be placed on standby lists or waitlists ahead of non-status passengers when multiple displaced travelers are competing for the same limited seats.

What if Delta rebooks me on a flight I don't want?

You have the right to request a different rebooking option. If the automatic rebooking does not suit your schedule, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 or speak with a gate agent to explore alternatives. You may also request a full refund if you choose not to travel at all after a significant controllable delay, depending on your original fare type.

Will Delta compensate me with miles if I miss a connection due to their fault?

Delta sometimes offers SkyMiles as a goodwill gesture for significant disruptions, but this is not a guaranteed policy. If you feel your disruption warrants additional compensation beyond what was provided at the airport, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 after arriving at your destination to open a formal customer care review.

What happens to my checked items if I miss a Delta connection?

If your items were checked to your final destination, Delta will forward them on the next available flight — they do not need to be re-checked at the connecting airport. Confirm with the gate agent or luggage desk that your items have been tagged through to the final destination before reboarding.

Can I miss my connection on purpose to get a later flight?

Intentionally missing a connection — often called 'hidden city' ticketing — is against Delta's ticketing rules and can result in the cancellation of remaining segments and restrictions on your SkyMiles account. It is not a recommended strategy and carries real consequences.
