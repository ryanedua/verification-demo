Delta Airlines Seat Upgrade Guide: Every Option Explained

Delta offers multiple upgrade pathways, from complimentary Medallion queue upgrades to purchased seat improvements using SkyMiles or cash. Understanding how each works, when they open, and how waitlists are prioritized determines whether you sit in Main Cabin or enjoy extra space and premium service at the front of the plane.

The Three Premium Cabins and What You Are Upgrading To

Delta operates three primary premium cabin products, each a meaningful step above the standard Main Cabin experience.

Comfort+ is the first upgrade above Main Cabin, available on most domestic and some international flights. Comfort+ seats provide additional legroom (up to four inches more than Main Cabin), dedicated overhead bin space, enhanced snack and beverage service, and priority boarding. On longer domestic routes, Comfort+ passengers also receive early access to in-flight entertainment. This is the most attainable upgrade for most travelers and the most common destination for Medallion waitlist upgrades.

First Class is available on domestic and short-haul international routes. First Class features a wider seat with more legroom and recline, a full meal service on flights of appropriate length, complimentary alcoholic beverages, dedicated overhead bin space, and Zone 1 boarding. On many domestic routes, First Class is a genuine upgrade in meal quality and seat comfort over Main Cabin.

Delta One is Delta's international business class product on long-haul routes. Delta One features a fully lie-flat seat, direct aisle access, premium dining, a dedicated flight attendant ratio, and in-flight amenity kits. Delta One is also available on certain premium domestic routes. Reaching Delta One through an upgrade — whether by SkyMiles, certificates, or Medallion eligibility — represents the most significant comfort improvement in the program. Medallion Complimentary Upgrades: How They Work

Medallion members receive complimentary upgrade eligibility as part of their status — but "eligible" does not mean "guaranteed." The upgrade is placed on a waitlist that clears based on tier, fare class, and the timing of the request.

Upgrade window timing by tier: Diamond Medallion members can request complimentary upgrades to domestic First Class at booking — many months before departure. Platinum Medallion members gain access at booking as well, though with slightly lower priority than Diamond. Gold Medallion members receive upgrade eligibility 120 days before departure. Silver Medallion members receive eligibility 72 hours before departure.

Within each tier, the waitlist is further ordered by the original fare class — a Gold Medallion passenger in a higher-priced Main Cabin fare has priority over a Gold Medallion passenger in the lowest-cost Main Cabin fare. Full-fare purchases generate the most favorable waitlist position within the same Medallion tier.

Waitlists clear continuously from the point of eligibility through gate closure. An upgrade that does not clear during the booking window may still clear 24 hours before departure, at check-in, or at the gate. Check the Fly Delta app for your current waitlist position number. If the flight is overbooked in premium cabin and the waitlist is long, even Diamond Medallion members may not clear on busy routes. For high-priority trips, consider purchasing a paid upgrade or First Class ticket directly rather than relying on the waitlist.

Purchasing Upgrades with SkyMiles or Cash

Any SkyMiles member — not just Medallion members — can purchase upgrades when inventory is available through two main options.

Pay with Miles: This option converts SkyMiles into a direct upgrade on qualifying flights when available upgrade space exists. The mileage cost varies dynamically, similar to how award ticket prices fluctuate with demand. Pay with Miles upgrades are confirmed immediately — no waitlist, no uncertainty. Check pricing under "Manage My Trip" on delta.com or in the Fly Delta app for your specific flight.

Cash upgrades: Delta sometimes offers cash upgrade bids or flat-rate upgrade options for available premium cabin seats, displayed in Manage My Trip as a fixed dollar amount. These are available to all fare classes that qualify for upgrades (not Basic Economy) and are confirmed immediately at checkout.

Systemwide Upgrade Certificates (SWUs): Issued to Platinum and Diamond Medallion members annually, SWUs can be applied to the upgrade waitlist for any Delta-operated flight, including international routes. SWUs do not confirm an upgrade — they improve your waitlist priority — so demand on the specific flight still determines whether the upgrade clears.

The most reliable path to a premium cabin seat is purchasing it directly as part of the original booking. Upgrades — whether purchased or waitlisted — become less available as departure approaches and other passengers finalize premium bookings. Calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 on the day of or day before travel gives you the most current picture of available upgrade inventory.

Checking Upgrade Status and What to Do at the Gate

Upgrade status can change at any point between your initial request and gate closure. The best tools to monitor it are the Fly Delta app and delta.com's Manage My Trip page, both of which update in real time.

In the app, navigate to your trip and look for the upgrade status section. The waitlist shows your current position number and the number of members ahead of you. This number changes as other passengers check in, as upgrade space opens or closes, and as Delta adjusts premium inventory allocation close to departure.

Check-in time (24 hours before departure) is a critical upgrade processing window. Many Medallion complimentary upgrades that have been sitting on the waitlist for weeks clear at the 24-hour mark when unsold premium cabin seats become available in the system. Check in through the app exactly when the 24-hour window opens.

At the gate on the day of travel, introduce yourself to the gate agent if you are near the top of the upgrade waitlist. Gate agents manage the final round of upgrade processing and can confirm waitlist movements as no-shows and last-minute changes free up seats. They can also process payment for a remaining paid upgrade seat if one opens and you decide to purchase it. Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get a free upgrade on Delta?

Complimentary upgrades on Delta are available to Medallion members based on tier and availability. Diamond Medallion members get the earliest and highest-priority access to upgrade waitlists, while Silver Medallion members receive access 72 hours before departure. Any member can also check for SkyMiles-based Pay with Miles upgrades, which are confirmed immediately. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to check live upgrade availability on your flight.

How do I check my upgrade waitlist position?

Open the Fly Delta app, navigate to your trip, and look for the upgrade status section. Your waitlist position number and the number of members ahead of you are displayed. This updates in real time as passengers check in and upgrade space changes. The position can also be viewed on delta.com under Manage My Trip.

Can I use SkyMiles to upgrade to Delta One?

Yes — SkyMiles can be used to upgrade to Delta One on qualifying international routes when upgrade space is available. The mileage cost depends on the route and demand. Platinum and Diamond Medallion members can apply systemwide upgrade certificates to the Delta One waitlist. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to check current upgrade inventory and mileage cost for your specific international route.

Can Basic Economy passengers upgrade on Delta?

Basic Economy tickets are not eligible for any upgrade — SkyMiles-based, Medallion-based, or cash. If you hold a Basic Economy ticket and want upgrade eligibility, you would need to rebook in a higher fare class first.

When does Delta process upgrades for Medallion members?

The timing depends on Medallion tier. Diamond and Platinum Medallion members can request upgrades at booking. Gold Medallion members gain access 120 days before departure, and Silver Medallion members at 72 hours before departure. Additional upgrade processing occurs at the 24-hour check-in window and at the gate on departure day.

What is the Pay with Miles upgrade option on Delta?

Pay with Miles is a program that allows any SkyMiles member to purchase a confirmed upgrade to Comfort+ or First Class using SkyMiles when space is available. The mileage price varies dynamically. Unlike Medallion upgrade waitlists, Pay with Miles upgrades are confirmed immediately upon purchase. Check Manage My Trip on delta.com for current Pay with Miles pricing, or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181.

Can I upgrade a companion using my Medallion status?

Medallion upgrade eligibility typically applies to the Medallion member's ticket. Some tiers allow upgrade certificates to be applied to a companion on the same booking. Delta SkyMiles Reserve Amex cardholders receive an annual companion certificate that can be used alongside the primary cardholder's upgrade. Confirm the specific terms for your status with Delta.

What happens if my upgraded seat is removed by Delta?

If Delta downgrades you from a purchased or confirmed upgrade due to an aircraft change or oversale, you are entitled to a refund of the upgrade cost — either the SkyMiles used or the cash paid. Contact Delta at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 immediately if this occurs, and have your confirmation number and original upgrade payment details ready.

Does upgrading to First Class change my SkyMiles earnings?

SkyMiles earned on a flight are based on the originally purchased fare class, not the upgraded cabin. Upgrading via a waitlist or complimentary certificate earns miles at the original ticket's rate. Purchasing a separate First Class fare at booking earns miles at the First Class earn rate.

How far in advance can I request an upgrade on Delta?

Diamond and Platinum Medallion members can request upgrades at the time of booking, which can be months in advance of travel. General members can check Pay with Miles availability at any time after booking. For most upgrade programs, the earlier you request, the better your position on the waitlist.
