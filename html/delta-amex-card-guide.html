The Three Delta SkyMiles Amex Card Tiers

Delta and American Express offer three primary co-branded consumer cards, each with a different annual fee and benefit set. There are also business versions of each tier, but this guide focuses on the personal cards.

Delta SkyMiles Gold American Express Card: The entry-level Delta Amex. Earns 2 SkyMiles per dollar on Delta purchases and at restaurants worldwide, and 1 mile per dollar on other purchases. Key benefits include a first checked item waived on Delta flights, priority boarding in Group 1, and 20% back on Delta in-flight purchases. Annual fee is in the lower tier of co-branded airline cards. Best for occasional Delta travelers who want to earn miles on everyday spending and occasionally fly Delta.

Delta SkyMiles Platinum American Express Card: The mid-tier card. Earns 3 miles per dollar on Delta purchases, 2 miles at restaurants and at U.S. supermarkets, and 1 mile on other spending. Adds a domestic companion certificate each year after renewal, a limited number of complimentary Delta Sky Club visits annually, Global Entry or TSA PreCheck fee credit, and increased MQD earning from card spending toward Medallion requalification. Best for regular Delta travelers who fly multiple times per year and can use the companion certificate.

Delta SkyMiles Reserve American Express Card: The premium tier. Earns 3 miles per dollar on Delta purchases and 1.5 miles on other eligible purchases above a spending threshold. Key benefits include unlimited Delta Sky Club access when flying same-day Delta flights, two complimentary Sky Club guest visits per entry, an annual companion certificate (including First Class routing), the highest MQD contribution from card spending, and the ability to earn Delta Medallion status faster. Annual fee is the highest of the three tiers. Best for frequent Delta travelers who value Sky Club access, are pursuing Medallion status, and regularly travel with a companion. How Delta Amex Cards Accelerate Medallion Status

Every Delta SkyMiles American Express card contributes Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs) that count toward annual Medallion status, up to a per-card annual cap. This is one of the most strategically important features of the co-branded cards — it reduces the amount of Delta flight spending needed to reach a Medallion tier.

The MQD contribution works as follows: eligible card purchases earn MQDs at a rate specified in the card terms, up to an annual cap that varies by card tier. The Gold card provides the lowest annual MQD cap, the Platinum card a higher cap, and the Reserve card the highest cap available through any Delta Amex card. Once the cap is reached, additional card spending does not generate more MQDs for that qualification year — but it continues to earn SkyMiles at the standard card rates.

The value of card MQDs becomes most apparent when you are close to a Medallion threshold as December 31 approaches. A Reserve cardholder who has accumulated significant flight MQDs but falls slightly short of Platinum's $15,000 threshold can use card spending in the final months of the year to close the gap without purchasing additional airline tickets. This flexibility makes the Reserve card particularly valuable for travelers who are close to a status level but cannot easily add more flights.

MQDs from card spending post to your SkyMiles account alongside flight MQDs. They appear in the same account dashboard under the Medallion progress tracker. If you believe card MQDs have not posted correctly, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 — agents can cross-reference your Amex statement activity against your SkyMiles account to identify any discrepancy.

Making Sense of Sign-Up Bonuses and Annual Benefits

Sign-up bonus miles are the most dramatic single-event mile accumulation most travelers experience, and understanding how they work ensures you receive the full offer.

Each Delta Amex card offers a welcome bonus that requires meeting a minimum spending threshold within a defined period after account opening — typically three months. The bonus amount and minimum spend vary by card tier and by current promotional offers. The spending must be on eligible purchases — some card terms exclude annual fees, interest charges, and balance transfers from the calculation.

Welcome bonuses post to your SkyMiles account as a single credit, typically within six to twelve weeks of meeting the spending requirement. If the bonus has not appeared after twelve weeks from when you met the minimum spend, contact American Express first, then Delta if Amex confirms the credit was transmitted but has not appeared in your SkyMiles account. The customer service line at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can open an inquiry with the SkyMiles partner credits team when needed.

Beyond the sign-up bonus, several ongoing annual benefits make a meaningful practical difference. The companion certificate — issued after each card renewal for Platinum and Reserve cardholders — is redeemable for a round-trip domestic flight (and select international routes for the Reserve card) for a companion traveling with the primary cardholder. The certificate requires only payment of taxes and fees for the companion's ticket, which can represent $400–$1,000+ in flight value depending on the route. Use the companion certificate on a route where the companion fare would otherwise be expensive — the highest-value use is on a route you would take anyway with a travel partner.

Earning SkyMiles on Card Spending: What Actually Posts

Card-earned SkyMiles follow a posting cycle tied to your monthly statement, not a real-time transaction system. Understanding this cycle prevents unnecessary concern when miles do not appear immediately after a purchase.

Purchases made during a billing cycle accumulate as pending rewards until the monthly statement closes. Once the statement generates, Amex transfers the earned SkyMiles to your Delta account, where they typically appear within two to three business days of the statement date. Purchases made the day before your statement closes appear in the next statement cycle — this can make it seem like a card purchase did not earn miles when it is actually just in the subsequent cycle.

Category-specific earnings — bonus miles at restaurants, supermarkets, or Delta purchases — depend on the merchant being coded in the correct category by the card network. A restaurant inside an airport may be coded as airport services rather than dining, for example, missing the dining bonus. Review your Amex rewards summary within the app to see how each transaction was categorized and at what earn rate the miles were awarded.

If a card earning discrepancy persists after a full statement cycle — a welcome bonus not appearing after twelve weeks, category bonuses missing from multiple statements, or a significant difference between expected and actual miles — the resolution path is: first contact Amex for an account review; second, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to have Delta check whether a credit file was received from Amex and applied to your SkyMiles account. Most posting discrepancies are resolved within ten business days once both companies are engaged on the inquiry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Delta Amex card is best for earning SkyMiles?

The Delta SkyMiles Reserve Amex earns the most miles on Delta purchases (3 miles per dollar) and provides the highest MQD contribution toward Medallion status. For travelers who also want Sky Club access and value the companion certificate, the Reserve is the top-tier option. For occasional Delta travelers, the Gold or Platinum card offers strong value at a lower annual fee. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to see how each card's benefits align with your travel patterns.

How long does it take for Delta Amex card miles to post?

Card miles post to your SkyMiles account within two to three business days after your monthly statement closes. Welcome bonus miles post within six to twelve weeks of meeting the spending requirement. If miles are past these timelines, contact Amex first, then call Delta if the issue is on the SkyMiles transfer side.

Does the Delta Amex companion certificate expire?

Yes — the companion certificate issued after card renewal is valid for a limited period specified in the certificate terms, typically one year from issuance. Use it within the validity period on a route that provides the most value. The certificate applies to round-trip domestic flights and, for Reserve cardholders, select First Class routing on certain routes.

Do Delta Amex card purchases earn Medallion Qualification Dollars?

Yes — eligible Delta Amex card purchases earn MQDs that count toward annual Medallion status, up to a card-specific annual cap. The Reserve card has the highest cap, making it the most effective card for accelerating Medallion requalification through spending. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to confirm how much MQD credit your card activity has contributed this qualification year.

Can I have more than one Delta Amex card?

American Express limits each person to one co-branded Delta card of each type — personal Gold, personal Platinum, personal Reserve, and their business equivalents. Holding multiple personal cards at different tiers simultaneously is not permitted. Each card's sign-up bonus also has time restrictions on earning it again after holding the same card.

What happens to my SkyMiles if I cancel my Delta Amex card?

SkyMiles already transferred to your SkyMiles account remain there after canceling the card — they do not expire on card closure. The card's ongoing benefits (Sky Club access, MQD contribution, companion certificate) end when the card is canceled. Miles already in the SkyMiles account are fully intact and spendable.

Is the Delta Reserve Amex worth the annual fee?

The Reserve card's worth depends on how you use its benefits. Unlimited Sky Club access alone is valued at $500–$700+ per year for frequent travelers. The companion certificate typically represents $400–$1,000+ in companion ticket value if used on an expensive route. If you use both benefits regularly and pursue Medallion status, the Reserve card typically returns well above its annual fee. If you rarely fly Delta or visit lounges infrequently, a lower-tier card provides better value.

Can I earn SkyMiles on international purchases with a Delta Amex card?

Yes — Delta Amex cards earn SkyMiles on purchases outside the U.S. at the card's standard earn rate. Note that while the Gold card charges a foreign transaction fee, the Platinum and Reserve cards do not, making them more suitable for international spending. Check your specific card's terms for the foreign transaction fee and earn rate on international purchases.

How do I apply a Delta Amex companion certificate?

When booking on delta.com, select a qualifying flight and proceed to payment. On the payment page, look for the companion certificate option and enter the certificate code from your email or Amex account. The companion's ticket is then issued for taxes and fees only. The companion must travel on the same itinerary as the primary cardholder.

What if my Delta Amex welcome bonus miles never posted?

First verify you met the minimum spending requirement by reviewing your Amex statements for the required period. Then confirm the SkyMiles number on file with Amex matches your current active account. If both are correct and more than twelve weeks have passed since meeting the threshold, call Amex to open an investigation, then call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 if Amex confirms the credit was issued but it has not appeared in your SkyMiles account.
