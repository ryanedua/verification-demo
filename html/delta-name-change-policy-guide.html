Minor Name Corrections vs. Legal Name Changes

Delta distinguishes between two types of name issues, and the category determines the process, timeline, and potential cost.

Minor typographical corrections involve small errors that clearly still refer to the same person — a transposed letter ("Jonh" instead of "John"), a doubled letter missing ("Michell" instead of "Michelle"), a surname with a different number of spaces or hyphens, or a middle initial included on the ticket but absent from the presented ID. These are treated as corrections to an existing record, not a transfer of the ticket to a new person.

Legal name changes occur when a traveler's legal name has officially changed — through marriage, divorce, court order, or another legal process — and the name on their current ID no longer matches the name on their Delta ticket. These require documentation and may involve ticket reissuance rather than a simple record update.

The practical difference is significant. A minor correction can often be processed in minutes during a phone call or at an airport ticket counter, while a legal name change requires the traveler to provide legal documentation and may result in a different ticket number being issued. To determine which category your situation falls into and begin the appropriate process, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 with your booking confirmation number and the exact name currently on the ticket.

For minor corrections, you can also visit a Delta ticket counter at the airport, particularly if you are traveling soon and need the correction completed on the spot. Airport agents have the same systems as phone agents, and in-person interactions can sometimes speed up the process for urgent same-day requests.

Online self-service name correction is not available through delta.com — name changes of any type require a Delta agent to process them directly. This prevents unauthorized changes and ensures that every correction is reviewed by a human before the ticket record is updated.

TSA Secure Flight and Why Name Accuracy Matters

The TSA Secure Flight program requires airlines to submit passenger name information to the federal government for security vetting before travel. The name submitted must match the government-issued ID the passenger will use at the security checkpoint.

Minor discrepancies — a missing middle name, a hyphenated surname entered without the hyphen — generally do not trigger security issues and may be accepted at the checkpoint. However, a first name and last name that are fully transposed, a surname with completely different letters, or a nickname that bears no resemblance to the legal name on your ID may result in secondary screening or, in some cases, denied boarding.

Delta recommends verifying name accuracy at the time of booking by comparing the entered name character by character against your ID. If you book frequently, consider saving your traveler profile in your SkyMiles account — the pre-saved information auto-populates when booking and reduces manual entry errors.

International travel carries additional requirements. When flying internationally with a passport, the name on your ticket must match your passport exactly — not your driver's license, not a previous legal name. If your passport and driver's license reflect different names (a common situation after a legal name change), always use your passport as the reference document for international flights and call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to correct any Delta ticket that still reflects your old name.

Fees and What to Expect When Changing a Name

Delta's fee structure for name corrections depends on the type of change and the fare class of the ticket.

Minor typographical corrections that can be resolved without reissuing the ticket are typically processed at no charge. The correction updates the existing record, and no new ticket number is generated. There is no fee to correct a transposed letter or a missing character when the change clearly involves the same person and is handled well in advance of departure.

Legal name changes that require ticket reissuance are more complex. The fare rules of the original ticket apply to the reissuance — if the ticket was non-refundable and there is now a higher fare available for the same route, the reissuance may require payment of the fare difference. Refundable tickets reissue without a fare difference concern.

Same-day corrections at the airport — particularly for corrections discovered within hours of departure — are possible but depend on the complexity of the change and agent availability. Corrections that require reissuance may not be completable same-day if the systems need extended processing time. The safest approach is to identify and correct any name issues at least 72 hours before departure. Calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 as soon as a name error is discovered — regardless of how far in advance the flight is — gives you the most options and avoids the compressed timeline and limited options of a same-day correction request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Delta fix a misspelled name on my ticket?

Yes — Delta corrects minor typographical errors where the name clearly refers to the same person. Call Delta's customer service team with your booking confirmation number and the corrected spelling exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID. Most minor corrections are processed the same day.

Is there a fee for a Delta name correction?

Minor corrections that do not require ticket reissuance are typically fee-free. Legal name changes requiring full reissuance may involve a service charge or fare difference depending on the ticket type and timing. Call the Delta support team for a specific cost estimate before proceeding with a name change.

What documents do I need for a legal name change on Delta?

For a legal name change, Delta requires official documentation establishing the new name — a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court order — along with the updated government-issued ID you will use for travel. Have these documents ready when you call Delta to begin the process.

How early should I fix a name error on a Delta ticket?

Fix name errors as soon as you discover them. Corrections made 72 or more hours before departure have the most processing time and the most options. Same-day corrections are possible but are more limited and depend on complexity. Do not wait until you are at the airport — call as soon as you identify the issue.

Can Delta change a first name and last name that are reversed?

A reversed first and last name is more complex than a typographical correction — it may require ticket reissuance rather than a simple record update. Call Delta customer care to have an agent assess whether the reversal can be corrected on the existing ticket or requires reissuance, and to understand any fees that apply.

Will a name correction affect my seat assignment?

A minor correction that does not require ticket reissuance preserves all seat assignments, upgrades, and ancillary purchases. If the ticket must be reissued for a legal name change, the agent processing the reissuance will carry over all existing seat selections and associated purchases where possible.

What if the name on my ticket does not exactly match my passport?

For international flights, the passport is the controlling document. Any discrepancy between your ticket name and your passport name should be corrected before travel. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 as soon as you identify the mismatch — even small differences can create delays at international check-in if the airline's Secure Flight submission does not match your passport.

Can I change the name on a Delta ticket to a different person?

Delta does not allow tickets to be transferred to a different person. A name change that effectively replaces one traveler with another is not a correction — it requires canceling the original ticket (subject to fare rules) and purchasing a new ticket for the correct traveler.

What if my Delta ticket was booked through a travel agency?

Tickets issued by travel agencies must be corrected through the issuing agency in many cases. However, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 lets a Delta agent assess whether Delta can process the correction directly or whether it needs to go through the agency. Acting quickly is important regardless of which channel is used.

How do I prevent name errors on future Delta bookings?

Save your traveler profile in your SkyMiles account with your legal name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID. When booking through delta.com while signed in, the profile auto-populates the passenger name fields. Review the name on your confirmation email immediately after booking and before the 24-hour cancellation window closes.
