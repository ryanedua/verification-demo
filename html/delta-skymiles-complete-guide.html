How to Earn Delta SkyMiles

SkyMiles are earned through multiple channels, and combining them is the fastest way to build a large balance.

Flying Delta: Every Delta-operated flight earns SkyMiles based on the fare paid and the fare class. Miles earned range from a base rate for economy tickets up to significantly higher rates for premium cabins. Medallion members earn bonus miles on top of the base earn rate — Diamond Medallion members can earn up to 11 miles per dollar spent on qualifying Delta flights.

Delta SkyMiles credit cards: Delta co-branded American Express cards earn SkyMiles on everyday spending. Depending on the card tier, you earn bonus miles at restaurants, at U.S. supermarkets, on hotel purchases, and on all Delta purchases. The Delta SkyMiles Reserve Amex card also provides Sky Club access and complimentary companion certificates annually.

Partner earning: SkyMiles are earned on flights with SkyTeam partner airlines — including Air France, KLM, Korean Air, and Virgin Atlantic — when your SkyMiles number is attached to the booking. Partner flights earn at rates set by the individual partner carrier, which are often lower than Delta's own earn rates. SkyMiles also accumulate through hotel stays, car rentals, and shopping portal purchases made through the SkyMiles shopping hub.

To verify that miles posted correctly after any earning activity, check the Activity tab in your SkyMiles account on delta.com within five business days. If miles are missing, file a retroactive claim online or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 for assistance.

Redeeming SkyMiles for Flights and Upgrades

SkyMiles can be redeemed for award tickets on Delta-operated flights and select partner airline routes, as well as for cabin upgrades on qualifying fares.

Award flights: Delta uses a dynamic pricing model for award tickets, meaning the mileage cost of a specific flight changes based on demand and availability — similar to how cash fares fluctuate. There is no fixed award chart for most routes. Flexible travel dates significantly expand the range of mileage costs available. Use the calendar view on delta.com to compare award pricing across multiple dates at once.

Upgrade redemptions: SkyMiles can be used to upgrade from Main Cabin to Comfort+, First Class, or — on qualifying international routes — Delta One business class. The Pay with Miles option allows any SkyMiles member to purchase a confirmed upgrade when space is available. Medallion members also receive upgrade waitlist priority.

Other redemptions: SkyMiles can be applied toward any Delta ticket through the "Shop with Miles" option at checkout, which offsets the cash price at a rate of approximately 1 cent per mile (values vary by fare and route). This option is best used when mileage rates on award tickets are elevated.

To check current award availability on specific flights or explore upgrade options, use the award search tool on delta.com or call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and have an agent pull live inventory for your preferred route and dates.

SkyMiles Expiration, Account Maintenance, and Security

One of the most traveler-friendly features of the SkyMiles program is that miles do not expire based on time alone. As long as your account remains active — defined as any mileage earn or redemption activity within a calendar year — your full balance is preserved indefinitely.

Account inactivity is the primary cause of mile expiration. If your account has had no activity for 24 consecutive months, Delta may consider the balance subject to expiration under certain program terms. The simplest way to maintain account activity is to make a small purchase through the SkyMiles shopping hub, to rent a car through a SkyMiles partner, or to earn miles through a Delta Amex card purchase.

Account security is another important consideration. SkyMiles accounts are a frequent target for unauthorized access because the miles have real monetary value. Delta recommends using a unique, strong password for your SkyMiles account and enabling any available two-step verification. If you notice unauthorized redemptions or suspicious account activity, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 immediately — Delta's security team can freeze the account, reverse unauthorized transactions, and reissue any miles that were removed without your authorization.

If you need to merge two SkyMiles accounts — for example, if a separate account was created accidentally — Delta handles account consolidations through a formal request process. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and have both account numbers and the associated email addresses ready to begin the merge request.

Maximizing SkyMiles Value Across the Program

Getting the most value from SkyMiles requires understanding a few key principles about how the program is structured.

Earn aggressively in bonus categories: Delta SkyMiles credit cards offer multiplied earning on Delta purchases, dining, and hotel stays. Concentrating spending in these categories builds miles faster than using a generic rewards card. The Delta Reserve Amex earns 3 miles per dollar on Delta purchases, which compounds quickly for frequent flyers.

Redeem at low-demand windows: Award pricing is dynamic, so mileage costs on popular routes during peak travel periods are substantially higher than off-peak dates. Booking a flight on a Tuesday or Wednesday, or in early January versus late December, often reveals significantly lower mileage requirements for the same route.

Use miles for premium cabins on long routes: The cents-per-mile value of a SkyMiles redemption is typically highest when applied to business class awards on long international flights, where the cash price of the ticket is disproportionately high. A Delta One award to Europe or Asia can represent 4–6 cents per mile in value — far above the approximate 1.1 cents per mile value when using Shop with Miles on a domestic economy ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Delta SkyMiles expire?

Delta SkyMiles do not expire as long as your account has at least one earning or redemption activity per calendar year. Inactive accounts with no activity for an extended period may have miles subject to expiration under Delta's program rules. To keep your account active, earn miles through any SkyMiles partner, including hotel stays or car rentals. Call Delta's customer service team if you are concerned about account status.

How many SkyMiles do I need for a free flight?

Delta uses dynamic award pricing, so the mileage cost for a specific flight depends on demand, route, and travel date rather than a fixed chart. Domestic round-trip awards can range from a few thousand miles on low-demand dates to tens of thousands on peak travel days. Check delta.com for current award pricing on your route, or call the Delta support team for live availability.

How do I check my SkyMiles balance?

Log in to delta.com and your SkyMiles balance appears on the account dashboard. The Fly Delta app also displays your balance on the home screen when you are signed in. The Activity tab shows a detailed history of all earning and redemption transactions with dates and amounts.

Can I transfer SkyMiles to another person?

SkyMiles are non-transferable between member accounts. However, your miles can be used to book award tickets for other people — the account holder redeems the miles, but the ticket can be issued in another passenger's name. For group bookings or family travel, call Delta customer care to explore the most efficient way to apply your SkyMiles balance.

What is the best way to earn SkyMiles quickly?

The fastest ways to accumulate SkyMiles include meeting the welcome bonus spending threshold on a Delta Amex card (which can add 40,000–100,000+ miles), flying Delta in a premium cabin, shopping through the SkyMiles shopping hub, and staying at hotel partners like Marriott or Hilton when those properties offer SkyMiles. Stack multiple earning sources simultaneously for the fastest accumulation.

How do I redeem SkyMiles for an upgrade?

To upgrade using SkyMiles, go to your booking in Manage My Trip on delta.com and look for 'Upgrade Options.' The Pay with Miles option shows current upgrade pricing for available premium cabins on your flight. Medallion members can also apply upgrade certificates. For complex itineraries or international upgrade availability, call Delta.

Can I earn SkyMiles on flights booked with SkyMiles?

No — award tickets redeemed with SkyMiles do not earn additional SkyMiles on the flight itself. You fly on a complimentary ticket using your existing miles, but the journey does not generate new mileage credit. If earning miles matters, purchase a paid fare instead of redeeming an award for that trip.

How do SkyMiles work with partner airlines?

Delta SkyMiles can be earned on flights with SkyTeam partner airlines when your SkyMiles number is attached at booking. Earn rates are set by the partner carrier and vary by fare class. SkyMiles can also be redeemed for partner airline award tickets, though availability and pricing are controlled by the partner. Attach your SkyMiles number to every partner booking to avoid missing retroactive credit opportunities.

What happens to my SkyMiles if I close my Delta Amex card?

SkyMiles earned through a Delta Amex card remain in your SkyMiles account after the card is closed — they do not expire upon card closure. The miles are yours regardless of the card's status, as long as your SkyMiles account itself remains active. Closing the card removes ongoing earning ability but does not affect the existing balance.

Can I buy SkyMiles if my balance is too low for an award?

Yes — Delta allows members to purchase additional SkyMiles to top up a balance, up to a maximum per year. Purchased miles cost more per mile than their typical redemption value, so this option is best used to close a small gap toward a specific high-value redemption. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 or visit delta.com to see current pricing for purchased miles.
