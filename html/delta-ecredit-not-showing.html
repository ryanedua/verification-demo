Delta eCredit Not Showing in Your Account — How to Fix It

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to have a Delta agent search your account for missing eCredits directly in the booking system — they can locate credits tied to your name even if they do not appear in your online wallet. A Delta eCredit is issued whenever a non-refundable ticket is canceled, a flight schedule changes significantly, or a fare adjustment creates a ticket credit. They appear in your SkyMiles account under 'My Wallet,' but several factors can cause them to be invisible or inaccessible through the standard online interface.

Why Your Delta eCredit May Not Be Showing

The most common reason an eCredit does not appear in your wallet is a mismatch between the name on the original ticket and the name on your current SkyMiles account. eCredits are tied to the passenger name on the booking, not just the SkyMiles number. If you have changed your name, updated your profile, or the ticket was booked under a slightly different spelling, the eCredit may exist in Delta's system but not be visible under your current login.

eCredits issued without a linked SkyMiles number — for example, when a ticket was purchased as a guest without signing in — are stored in Delta's system under the ticket number and passenger name alone. These credits are accessible but require the original ticket number to retrieve. Check your cancellation confirmation email for the 13-digit ticket number starting with 006.

A third possibility is that the eCredit has expired. eCredits issued in 2024 and later are typically valid for 12 months from the original ticket purchase date. If the ticket was purchased more than a year ago and you have not used or extended the credit, it may no longer be active. Delta has on occasion honored expired eCredits on a goodwill basis — calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 is the way to request a review.

Guest bookings — tickets purchased without signing in to a SkyMiles account — generate eCredits that are stored under the ticket number and passenger name rather than a loyalty account. To retrieve a guest eCredit, you do not need to be logged in at all. On delta.com, navigate to "My Trips," choose "Look up by confirmation number," and enter the original booking confirmation along with the passenger last name. The eCredit associated with that booking should appear in the trip details even without a SkyMiles account. If you want to consolidate the credit into your wallet for easier tracking, calling Delta and providing the ticket number allows an agent to link it to your account.

How to Find a Missing Delta eCredit

Start by logging in to delta.com and navigating to "My Account" → "My Wallet" → "eCredits." The wallet page shows all active eCredits associated with your SkyMiles account. If you do not see a credit you expected, check the following:

Search by ticket number: On the eCredits page, there is an option to look up a credit by ticket number. Enter the 13-digit number from your original booking confirmation. This bypasses the name-matching process and retrieves the credit directly from the ticket record.

Check the email address: eCredits may be linked to a different email address if the booking was made under an older email account. Search your archived email for "Delta cancellation confirmation" to find the original booking details.

Guest booking lookup: If the original ticket was purchased without a SkyMiles number, the eCredit is retrievable by providing the ticket number and the passenger's last name at checkout. You do not need to be logged in to apply a guest-issued eCredit to a new booking.

The Fly Delta app and the delta.com desktop site display eCredit information slightly differently. The app's "My Wallet" section may show a streamlined view that omits certain older credits or guest-issued certificates that are technically still valid. If an eCredit you expected is absent from the app, sign in on the full desktop site and check the wallet there — the desktop version typically surfaces the most complete record of all active and recently expired certificates tied to your account. If you find the credit on desktop but cannot locate it in the app, proceed with booking on desktop until the app display syncs after your next login.

Applying a Delta eCredit to a New Booking

Once you locate your eCredit, applying it to a new booking is straightforward. On delta.com, proceed through the booking flow until you reach the payment page. Select "eCredits / Gift Cards / Certificates" and enter the eCredit certificate number. The credit applies automatically toward the total fare, and any remaining balance can be paid with a credit card or SkyMiles.

eCredits can only be used on delta.com or through the Fly Delta app — they cannot be applied to bookings made through third-party travel sites. If you prefer to book over the phone, agents at +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 can apply an eCredit to a reservation and confirm the remaining balance during the call.

If an eCredit partially covers a ticket but you do not have a payment method to cover the difference, Delta agents can sometimes split the remaining balance across multiple eCredits if you have more than one on file. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to explore multi-credit application options before booking.

If you hold both a Delta eCredit and a Delta gift card, you can apply both toward the same booking during checkout. The payment page accepts eCredits and gift cards in separate fields — enter the eCredit certificate number in the eCredit field and the gift card number in the gift card field. After both are applied, any remaining balance can be paid with a credit card, debit card, or PayPal. The order in which you enter them does not affect the final amount, but entering the eCredit first allows the system to confirm its validity and remaining balance before you add the secondary payment method.

What to Do If Your eCredit Has Expired

Expired eCredits are not automatically reinstated, but Delta has a history of reviewing expiration situations on a case-by-case basis — particularly when the expiration occurred during a period of travel disruption or when there is a documented reason the credit went unused.

To request a review of an expired eCredit, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and provide the original ticket number, the expiration date, and the reason the credit was not used before it expired. The agent escalates the request to Delta's customer experience team, which handles goodwill exceptions.

While reinstatement is not guaranteed, Delta has been known to extend credits or convert them to a new form of voucher when there is a compelling reason. Providing documentation — such as a medical record, a Delta flight cancellation notice, or other evidence of unavoidable circumstance — strengthens the case. If the request is declined, ask the agent to document your account with the inquiry, as a subsequent call with additional information may lead to a different outcome.

If Delta declines your request to reinstate an expired eCredit during the first call, ask the agent to log the request on your account with a case number. Calling back with a case number from a prior inquiry signals to the next agent that the matter is already documented, which can streamline the follow-up review. Delta's customer experience team, which handles goodwill exception decisions, typically responds to escalated written requests within seven business days. Providing a concise written summary of why the credit went unused — including any corroborating documentation — submitted through Delta's feedback form on delta.com gives the team a record to reference when reviewing your file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Delta eCredit last?

eCredits issued in 2024 and later are valid for 12 months from the original ticket purchase date — not the cancellation date. If you are approaching the expiration date and cannot travel soon, call Delta's customer service team to ask whether an extension is possible under current Delta policies.

Can I use a Delta eCredit for someone else's ticket?

Delta eCredits are generally non-transferable — they must be used by the passenger whose name is on the original ticket. However, you can apply your own eCredit toward a ticket that includes you as a traveler, even if other passengers are on the same itinerary.

What happens if my eCredit doesn't cover the full ticket price?

You can pay the difference with a credit card, debit card, PayPal, or additional SkyMiles when the eCredit does not fully cover the fare. If you have multiple eCredits on file, call the Delta support team to request that an agent apply them in combination, as the online booking tool may only allow one at a time.

Can I get a cash refund instead of a Delta eCredit?

Non-refundable tickets automatically convert to eCredits upon cancellation. A cash refund for a non-refundable ticket is only available in specific circumstances — such as a Delta-initiated cancellation or within the 24-hour purchase window. Refundable tickets always return cash to the original payment method.

Why did my Delta eCredit disappear from my wallet?

An eCredit may disappear from your wallet if it expired, if it was already applied to another booking, or if a profile update caused a name mismatch. Call Delta customer care with your original ticket number and the agent can trace the credit in the reservation system and explain exactly what happened to it.

Can I use a Delta eCredit to book Basic Economy?

Yes — Delta eCredits can be applied toward any fare class, including Basic Economy. The credit covers the ticket cost; you pay any remaining balance with another accepted payment method. There are no fare-class restrictions on where an eCredit can be applied.

Can I combine a Delta eCredit with SkyMiles on the same booking?

Yes — Delta allows you to combine an eCredit with SkyMiles or a credit card on the same booking. Apply the eCredit first on the payment page, then select SkyMiles or a card for the remaining balance. For assistance combining payment methods, call Delta.

What if my eCredit was issued to the wrong SkyMiles account?

If an eCredit was issued to an incorrect SkyMiles number — for example, a digit was wrong at booking — call the Delta team immediately. Delta agents can search the credit by ticket number and passenger name and transfer it to your correct account after identity verification.

Is a Delta eCredit the same as a gift card?

No — a Delta eCredit is a travel certificate linked to a canceled ticket and can only be applied toward Delta flights on delta.com or the Fly Delta app. A Delta gift card is a prepaid card that can be used more broadly. The two are applied separately at checkout under different fields on the payment page.

Can I request an eCredit if I missed my Delta flight?

No-show situations — where you miss a flight without canceling ahead of time — generally result in forfeiture of the ticket value rather than an eCredit. Some fare types and circumstances allow an exception. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 as soon as possible after a missed flight to understand what options remain for your specific fare type.
