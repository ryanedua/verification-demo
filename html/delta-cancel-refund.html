Delta's 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation Window

Delta allows any passenger to cancel a reservation within 24 hours of purchase for a complete refund — no cancellation fee, no exceptions, no questions asked. This policy applies to all fare classes, including Basic Economy, provided the ticket was purchased at least seven days before the scheduled departure date.

To use this window, sign in to delta.com, go to "My Trips," select your reservation, and choose "Cancel." The refund processes automatically to the original form of payment. Credit card refunds typically appear within seven business days; debit card refunds can take up to 20 business days depending on your bank.

If you purchased through the Fly Delta app or by calling Delta directly, the same 24-hour protection applies. If you are uncertain whether the window is still open, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 lets an agent confirm your eligibility in seconds before you take any action. Acting before the window closes is always the cleanest path to a full cash refund.

Keep in mind that the 24-hour window starts at the exact timestamp of purchase, not at midnight of the day you bought the ticket. If you finalized the booking at 4:15 PM on Monday, the window closes at 4:15 PM on Tuesday — not at midnight Tuesday. Third-party platforms such as Expedia or Google Flights may apply their own intermediary cancellation policies that differ from Delta's, which is one reason purchasing directly through delta.com or the Fly Delta app gives you the cleanest access to Delta's standard 24-hour guarantee without additional terms layered on top.

Refundable vs. Non-Refundable Delta Tickets

Delta sells tickets in two broad categories, and the category your fare falls into determines what happens when you cancel outside the 24-hour window.

Refundable tickets are labeled "Refundable" at checkout. Canceling one at any point before departure returns the full ticket price to your original payment method — no partial credit, no conversion to an eCredit. These fares cost more upfront but eliminate financial risk entirely if your schedule changes.

Non-refundable tickets — the majority of Main Cabin, Comfort+, and some First Class fares — do not return cash after the 24-hour window passes. Instead, Delta issues a Delta eCredit equal to the remaining ticket value, valid for 12 months from the original purchase date and usable toward any future Delta booking.

Basic Economy is the most restrictive. Once the 24-hour window closes, Basic Economy tickets cannot be changed or canceled for any credit or refund. The full fare is forfeited. If there is any chance your plans will shift, Basic Economy is worth avoiding. To confirm which fare type you hold before canceling, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181.

One practical way to identify your fare type before calling is to look at the one- or two-letter fare basis code on your booking confirmation — codes beginning with Y, B, or M typically indicate refundable fares, while V, T, L, and Q are common non-refundable Main Cabin codes. This is not a definitive rule, as fare codes vary by route and booking class, but it gives you a useful starting point. When in doubt, reviewing the fare rules link on your booking confirmation page shows the full terms for your specific ticket before you take any action.

How to Cancel Your Delta Flight: Step by Step

Delta offers three cancellation channels, and the best one depends on how your ticket was originally purchased.

Online at delta.com: Sign in, navigate to "My Trips," select your flight, and click "Cancel Flight." The site shows you a summary of your refund amount or eCredit before you confirm — review it carefully before clicking the final button.

Fly Delta app: Open the app, tap "My Trips," select the trip, and tap "Cancel." The app sends a confirmation email immediately after processing.

By phone: Calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 connects you with a live Delta agent who can handle the cancellation manually. This is the best option for group bookings, itineraries booked through a travel agent, international tickets with complex routing, or situations where the online tool displays an error. Agents can also walk through the exact refund or eCredit amount with you before the cancellation is finalized, so there are no surprises.

Save the cancellation confirmation email Delta sends. It includes the cancellation reference number and, if applicable, the eCredit code — both are necessary if you need to follow up later.

Group bookings — those involving 10 or more passengers — follow a separate cancellation process through Delta's Group Desk rather than the standard self-service tools. The online cancellation button is typically disabled for group reservations, and changes need to be initiated by calling the Group Desk directly. If you booked a group itinerary and are unsure which team to contact, a general customer service agent at the main support line can route your call appropriately and note the group reservation code while connecting you to the correct team.

Tracking Your Delta Refund or eCredit

After canceling, the timeline for receiving your money back depends on the original payment method. Credit card refunds appear within seven business days. Debit card transactions can take up to 20 business days. PayPal refunds follow the standard seven-business-day timeline.

To check refund status, visit delta.com and use the "Check Refund Status" tool in the Help section. You need the 13-digit ticket number (starting with 006) and the last name on the booking.

eCredits appear in your SkyMiles account almost immediately after cancellation. To view them, sign in, go to "My Wallet," and select "eCredits." Each eCredit shows the amount, expiration date, and the original ticket number it was issued from.

If a refund or eCredit does not appear after the stated window, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181. Delta agents have direct access to the refund processing system and can escalate any delay to the finance team. Most unresolved refund issues are corrected within one additional billing cycle once an agent flags them internally.

Award tickets canceled within the 24-hour window receive a full refund of any taxes and fees paid, and the SkyMiles used for the booking are returned to your account immediately. For paid tickets, once you have submitted the cancellation request, save the confirmation email Delta sends — it contains the 13-digit ticket number starting with 006, which is the reference you will need if you ever have to follow up on a delayed refund or confirm an eCredit code. Most issues with refunds that appear overdue resolve quickly when you provide this number directly to a Delta agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel a Delta flight for free?

Yes — Delta's 24-hour policy allows free cancellation for any fare class within one day of purchase, as long as the flight is at least seven days out. Outside that window, free cancellation applies only to refundable fares. Non-refundable tickets convert to an eCredit. Call Delta's customer service team if you are unsure which option applies to your booking.

How long does a Delta refund take to process?

Credit card refunds typically take seven business days. Debit card refunds can take up to 20 business days depending on your bank. eCredits appear in your account almost immediately. If the refund has not posted after the stated window, call the Delta support team to request a status check and escalation if needed.

What happens to my SkyMiles if I cancel an award ticket?

Canceling a SkyMiles award ticket redeposits the miles to your account. Delta eliminated redeposit fees for most award cancellations made before departure. Taxes and fees paid are refunded to the original payment method. For partner award bookings or complex itineraries, calling Delta customer care confirms the redeposit is processed correctly.

Can I cancel a Basic Economy ticket on Delta?

Basic Economy tickets cannot be changed or canceled for credit or a refund once the 24-hour purchase window has passed. Within the first 24 hours of booking — provided the departure is at least seven days away — cancellation is still available at no cost.

What if Delta cancels my flight?

If Delta cancels your flight or makes a significant schedule change, you are entitled to a complete refund to your original payment method regardless of your fare type. You may also request rebooking on the next available Delta flight at no charge. Call Delta to confirm your refund eligibility and explore rebooking options when Delta initiates a cancellation.

How do I cancel a Delta flight booked through a third party?

Cancellations for third-party bookings must go through the original booking platform. If the platform is unresponsive, calling the Delta team lets a Delta agent note the situation and, in some cases, coordinate directly with the third-party provider. Acting quickly is important as deadlines may apply.

Can I get a refund after the 24-hour cancellation window?

After the window closes, cash refunds only apply to refundable fares. Non-refundable tickets convert to eCredits. In exceptional cases — documented medical emergencies, for example — Delta may process a refund exception on a case-by-case basis. Call Delta support and have supporting documentation available.

What is a Delta eCredit and how long does it last?

A Delta eCredit is the value of a canceled non-refundable ticket stored in your SkyMiles wallet. It can be applied toward any future Delta ticket purchase through delta.com or the Fly Delta app. eCredits issued in 2024 and later are generally valid for 12 months from the original ticket purchase date. To confirm your specific eCredit terms, call Delta's reservations team.

Can I cancel only one leg of a round-trip Delta ticket?

Yes — Delta allows cancellation of individual segments on a round-trip booking. Canceling one leg may affect the overall fare calculation and could result in a fare adjustment. Review the impact on the remaining segment before confirming any partial cancellation.

Does travel insurance cover Delta cancellation fees?

Trip cancellation insurance may reimburse non-refundable Delta fares if the reason qualifies under the policy — such as a covered illness or injury. Delta does not manage insurance claims. Explore all Delta refund options first, then file an insurance claim for any remaining non-recoverable amount.
