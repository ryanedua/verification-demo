How Delta Amex Card Miles Are Posted to SkyMiles

Miles earned through Delta SkyMiles American Express cards are processed by Amex and transferred to Delta's SkyMiles system. This transfer does not happen in real time — it typically occurs when your monthly statement closes. Purchases made mid-cycle appear as pending activity on your Amex account but are not transferred to your SkyMiles wallet until the statement generates.

Once the statement closes, the miles appear in your SkyMiles account within two to three business days. If your statement closed recently and the miles are not yet showing, the transfer may still be in process — allow the full three-business-day window before flagging the issue.

The SkyMiles account you want the miles posted to must be the one linked to your Delta Amex card. To confirm which SkyMiles number is on file with Amex, log in to your American Express account online, go to card benefits or rewards settings, and look for the SkyMiles number associated with the card. A mismatch here sends the miles to the wrong account or causes a posting failure.

Your Amex statement closing date is the key milestone in the mileage posting cycle. You can find it on your most recent Amex statement or in your online account under "Account Summary." Purchases made in the week before closing post as part of the current statement cycle, while purchases made immediately after closing roll into the next cycle. If you are trying to reach a spending threshold for a welcome bonus or promotional offer by a specific date, confirming the statement closing date in advance ensures your qualifying spending is processed within the correct billing period rather than falling into the next cycle.

Why Welcome Bonuses and Promotional Miles Take Longer

Welcome bonuses — the large mileage offers tied to meeting a spending threshold within the first few months of account opening — follow a separate timeline from regular purchase miles. Amex typically processes welcome bonuses within eight to twelve weeks of meeting the spending requirement, not at the next statement closing.

Before contacting Amex or Delta about a missing welcome bonus, verify two things: first, that you have actually met the spending requirement by checking your Amex statement total against the bonus terms; and second, that enough time has passed — if you met the threshold less than eight weeks ago, the bonus may still be on schedule.

Category bonuses — such as the additional SkyMiles earned on Delta purchases, at restaurants, or at U.S. supermarkets depending on your card tier — post at statement closing along with base miles. If a specific bonus category is missing, check that the merchant where you spent was coded in that eligible category. Merchants are classified by merchant category codes that are set by the card network, not by the merchant name — a restaurant inside an airport, for example, may be coded as an airport service rather than a dining establishment, missing the dining bonus.

Tracking your progress toward a welcome bonus spending requirement is straightforward through your Amex online account — the "Rewards" or "Welcome Offer" section typically shows the running total of qualifying purchases and the amount still needed to hit the threshold. Not all purchase types count toward the minimum spend: annual fees, cash advances, and returned purchases reduce the qualifying total. If you are close to the threshold and have recently returned items, recalculate your net qualifying spend rather than relying solely on the gross purchases displayed. A shortfall in qualifying spend by even one dollar means the full welcome bonus is not triggered.

Checking Your Amex Account vs. SkyMiles Account

Diagnose a missing miles issue by checking both accounts separately before contacting either company.

On your Amex account: Log in and check the Rewards section. This shows the total miles earned this statement cycle and the total transferred to SkyMiles year-to-date. If miles are showing in your Amex rewards balance but not in your SkyMiles account, the issue is in the transfer between Amex and Delta — contact Amex first.

On your SkyMiles account: Log in to delta.com and check the Activity tab. Filter by date range matching your recent statement period. Miles from Amex appear as "SkyMiles Credit Card" entries. If you see a credit that matches the expected amount but seems lower than you expected, check the earn rate for the specific purchase type — base rates vary by card tier and spending category.

If neither account shows any record of the miles, and the issue involves a welcome bonus or promotion, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and explaining that a credit card earning credit is missing allows a Delta agent to open an inquiry with the SkyMiles partner credits team, which liaises directly with Amex to resolve transfer discrepancies.

Some categories of purchases may earn a lower rate or no bonus miles at all, even when spent at a business type that sounds eligible. Supermarket purchases made at stores that are also general merchandise retailers — such as Target or Walmart, which have grocery sections — are typically coded by the card network as general merchandise rather than supermarkets, and may not earn the dining or supermarket bonus on your Delta Amex card. The earn rate for each individual charge is visible in the "Transaction Details" section of your monthly statement — reviewing this section helps identify any charges that were coded in an unexpected category before the statement closes.

Steps to Resolve Missing Delta Amex Miles

If the three-to-eight-week standard window has passed and the miles are still not in your SkyMiles account, here is the escalation path:

Step 1 — Contact American Express: Call the number on the back of your Delta Amex card and ask about the status of your SkyMiles credit. Amex customer care can see whether the miles were issued from their side and whether a transfer to Delta's system was initiated. Get a case number from Amex for any investigation they open.

Step 2 — Contact Delta SkyMiles: Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 with your SkyMiles number, the Amex case number from Step 1, and the approximate date and amount of the miles in question. A SkyMiles specialist can check whether a credit file was received from Amex and whether it was applied to your account or held in a pending queue.

Step 3 — Submit a formal inquiry: If both calls fail to resolve the issue, ask the Delta agent to submit a formal partner credit inquiry to the SkyMiles partner team. This team handles discrepancies between card issuers and SkyMiles directly, and most investigations are resolved within 10 business days.

When you open a formal partner credit inquiry with Delta's SkyMiles team, ask the agent to provide you with a reference number for the inquiry — this is separate from Amex's case number and is Delta's internal tracking code for the investigation with its partner teams. Having both case numbers ensures that if you need to follow up after 10 business days, each company can pull up the active case directly without re-explaining the situation from the start. Most partner credit inquiries involving clear documentation — a correct SkyMiles number on file and a confirmed Amex transfer — are resolved within the standard 10-business-day window without needing a second follow-up call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for Delta Amex card miles to post?

Standard purchase miles post to your SkyMiles account within two to three business days after your monthly statement closes. Welcome bonuses and promotional credits can take eight to twelve weeks after meeting the qualifying spending requirement. If your miles are past these windows, contact Amex first, then call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 if the issue is not resolved.

Why are my Delta Amex welcome bonus miles not showing up?

Welcome bonuses typically appear eight to twelve weeks after meeting the spending threshold, not at statement closing. Verify that you met the minimum spend by reviewing your Amex statement, then allow the full twelve weeks before escalating. Check that your SkyMiles number on file with Amex matches your active account.

What if my SkyMiles number is wrong on my Delta Amex card?

A wrong SkyMiles number on your Amex account means all miles are being sent to the incorrect account. Log in to your Amex account and update the SkyMiles number in the rewards settings, or call Amex customer care for assistance. For help recovering miles already sent to the wrong account, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 after correcting the number.

Do Delta Amex miles expire?

SkyMiles earned through Delta Amex card spending do not expire as long as your SkyMiles account remains active. Account activity — earning or redeeming any miles — is sufficient to keep the account current. Your Delta Amex account being open also qualifies as activity in most scenarios.

Can I earn bonus SkyMiles at restaurants with a Delta Amex card?

Certain Delta SkyMiles Amex cards earn bonus miles at U.S. restaurants. However, restaurant earnings depend on the merchant being coded in the dining category by the card network. Restaurants inside airports or hotel properties may be coded differently, which can affect bonus earning. Check your Amex statement's reward details to see the earn rate applied to a specific charge.

What is the difference in earning rates between Delta Amex cards?

The Delta SkyMiles Gold, Platinum, and Reserve Amex cards each have different earning structures. Higher-tier cards earn more miles per dollar on Delta purchases, dining, and other categories. The Reserve card also provides access to the Delta Sky Club. Review your specific card's benefits guide on the Amex website for the exact miles-per-dollar breakdown.

Can I transfer Delta Amex miles to another SkyMiles account?

SkyMiles are generally non-transferable between accounts. Once miles post to your SkyMiles account, they stay there. If the miles posted to the wrong account due to an incorrect SkyMiles number on file, contact both Amex and Delta to initiate a correction, as recovery requires both companies to cooperate.

Do Delta Amex card purchases earn MQDs for Medallion status?

Yes — eligible Delta Amex card purchases earn Medallion Qualification Dollars (MQDs) that count toward your annual Medallion requalification, up to the annual cap set by your specific card tier. MQDs from Amex card spending appear in your SkyMiles account alongside flight-earned MQDs. For questions about your current MQD total, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181.

What if I returned a purchase — do the miles get taken back?

Yes — if you return a purchase that earned SkyMiles, Amex will deduct the corresponding miles when the return processes on your statement. This is a standard rewards adjustment. If miles are deducted but you did not make the return, contact Amex to contest the charge adjustment.

Can I combine Delta Amex miles with miles I earn from flying?

Yes — all SkyMiles, whether earned from flights or from Delta Amex card spending, pool together in your single SkyMiles account. They can be redeemed together toward the same award ticket or upgrade without any separation between the two earning sources.
