How to Correct a Name Error on Your Delta Ticket

Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 as soon as you notice a name error on your Delta ticket — name corrections must match your government-issued ID exactly by the time you check in, and TSA boarding requirements leave no room for significant mismatches. Delta distinguishes between minor typographical corrections and legal name changes, and the process and fees differ accordingly. Acting quickly — ideally more than 72 hours before departure — gives you the most options and avoids last-minute complications at the airport.

What Counts as a Minor Name Correction on Delta

Delta classifies a name correction as minor when the change involves a small typographical error and the corrected name still clearly refers to the same person. Common examples include: a single letter transposed or swapped (e.g., "Jonhson" → "Johnson"), a double letter missing (e.g., "Anne" → "Anne"), a missing middle initial that appears on your ID, or a hyphenated name entered without the hyphen.

Minor corrections are generally permitted without replacing the ticket entirely. Delta's policy allows corrections that do not effectively change who the ticket belongs to — a first and last name swap is typically not permitted, nor is a change to an entirely different name. The corrected name must match your travel document exactly as it will be presented at check-in and at the security checkpoint.

To request a minor correction, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181. The agent will ask for your booking confirmation number, the name as it currently appears on the ticket, and the corrected spelling exactly as it appears on your government-issued photo ID. Most minor corrections can be processed during the call, and Delta will send an updated booking confirmation to your email address shortly afterward.

If you discover the name error after arriving at the airport, go directly to the Delta ticket counter rather than the gate — ticket counter agents have more system access and more time to process name corrections before the flight closes. Gate agents can sometimes make corrections at the boarding gate as well, but the time pressure makes it less reliable, and there is no guarantee a gate agent has the authority to reissue a ticket with a name change at that stage. Arriving at least two hours before a domestic departure (three for international) gives you enough time to resolve a minor correction at the ticket counter without risking your boarding window.

Legal Name Changes on Delta Tickets

If your name has changed legally — due to marriage, divorce, court order, or other legal process — Delta treats the update as a legal name change rather than a typographical correction. This requires more documentation and may involve a different process than a simple typo fix.

To process a legal name change, you will need to contact Delta with a copy of the legal document establishing the new name — such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court order — along with your booking confirmation and the updated ID you will use for travel. Delta may require the ticket to be reissued, and fare differences or change fees may apply depending on the ticket type.

If you recently changed your legal name and are traveling soon, call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 immediately. The earlier you start this process, the more time Delta's team has to reissue the ticket and update all connecting reservations. Attempting a legal name change at the airport on departure day is not guaranteed to succeed, and the consequences of a failed update include being denied boarding.

When a legal name change requires ticket reissuance, the timeline depends on how many segments your itinerary includes. A simple round-trip domestic ticket can typically be reissued within 24 hours once Delta receives and verifies the required documentation. A multi-city itinerary involving connecting flights on both Delta-operated and partner-operated segments takes longer, as each carrier's system may need to be updated individually. For international trips involving a name change, build in at least 72 hours before departure to allow time for all carriers in the routing to update their passenger records and for the updated Secure Flight information to propagate to TSA.

TSA Name Matching Requirements

The Transportation Security Administration's Secure Flight program requires that the name on your boarding pass match the name on your government-issued photo ID exactly — or as close as the ID allows (the TSA accepts some variations, such as omitted middle names, but not transposed first and last names or significantly misspelled surnames).

A minor discrepancy — one letter off in a long surname, or an initial versus the full middle name — typically does not trigger a security issue. However, a first name and last name that are fully swapped, a surname that bears no resemblance to your ID, or a nickname in place of your legal name can result in secondary screening or denied boarding.

Delta enters your name into the Secure Flight system when you book, so corrections made before ticketing is finalized are simpler than corrections after the ticket is issued. If you need to confirm that your name is entered correctly in the Secure Flight record, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 lets an agent check the exact string that was submitted to TSA for your booking.

The timing of your name correction relative to Secure Flight submission matters. Airlines submit Secure Flight passenger records to TSA at least 72 hours before departure for most flights. A name correction made more than 72 hours before departure means the corrected information is submitted as part of the original record. A correction made after that 72-hour mark requires Delta to submit an updated record, which may take a few hours to process through TSA's system. In time-sensitive situations, confirming with a Delta agent that the corrected Secure Flight record has been submitted — not just that the booking has been updated — ensures no gap between what the airline says and what TSA sees.

Fees and Timelines for Delta Name Corrections

Delta's fee structure for name corrections depends on the type of correction and how close to departure the request is made. Minor typographical corrections — a letter transposed, a missing character — are often processed without a fee when requested well in advance and when the ticket does not need to be fully reissued.

Legal name changes that require ticket reissuance may trigger a change fee depending on the fare type and how much lead time remains before the flight. Refundable fares typically allow this without a fee; non-refundable fares may involve a service charge plus any fare difference if the current price of the route has changed since original purchase.

The clearest way to understand the exact cost before committing is to call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 and ask an agent to quote the correction fee for your specific situation. Agents can tell you whether the ticket needs to be reissued, what the fee will be, and how the updated ticket will be delivered to you. Corrections made more than 72 hours before departure are generally handled more smoothly than last-minute requests, which may have limited agent availability.

If you believe your name correction should be fee-free but are being quoted a charge, ask the agent to clarify whether the fee applies because the ticket is being reissued or because of the specific fare type's change rules. Minor corrections that do not require full reissuance — single character fixes, missing hyphens, or incorrect middle initial — should not incur a fee even for non-refundable tickets. If the agent confirms that the ticket does need to be reissued due to the extent of the correction, ask whether applying any available eCredits or travel certificates to the difference reduces the out-of-pocket cost before authorizing the change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Delta fix a misspelled name on a ticket?

Yes — Delta allows minor typographical corrections where a small error clearly identifies the same person as the traveler. Call Delta's customer service team with your booking confirmation number and the corrected spelling as it appears on your government-issued ID. Most simple corrections are handled during the call.

Is there a fee to correct a name on a Delta ticket?

Minor corrections that do not require ticket reissuance are often processed at no charge. Legal name changes or corrections that involve reissuing the ticket may carry a service fee depending on fare type and timing. Call the Delta support team to get a specific fee quote for your situation before requesting the change.

How close to departure can I correct a name on a Delta flight?

Name corrections can technically be requested up to check-in, but changes made less than 24 hours before departure are more difficult to process and may not reflect on all documents in time. Contact Delta as early as possible — ideally 72 hours or more before the flight — to ensure the correction is fully processed before you arrive at the airport.

Does TSA allow small name differences on a Delta boarding pass?

The TSA Secure Flight program accepts minor discrepancies — such as a missing middle name or a slightly different middle initial — but does not permit a first and last name swap or a name that is substantially different from your ID. Significant mismatches can result in secondary screening or denied boarding.

What if I booked a Delta flight under a nickname instead of my legal name?

A nickname that differs significantly from your legal name may need to be corrected before travel, particularly if your government ID does not reflect the nickname. Call Delta customer care to have the booking reviewed and corrected to your legal name as it appears on your travel document.

Can I change the name on a Delta ticket to a different person?

Delta does not allow tickets to be transferred to a different person. If the wrong person is listed as the traveler, the ticket must be canceled and a new ticket purchased for the correct individual. Standard cancellation rules and fees apply.

What documents do I need for a legal name change on a Delta ticket?

For a legal name change, Delta requires documentation such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court order establishing the new name, along with the updated government-issued ID you will present at the airport. Call +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 to begin the process and confirm exactly which documents are needed for your specific situation.

Can I correct a name error on a Delta ticket booked through a travel agency?

If your Delta ticket was booked through a travel agency or third-party platform, the correction typically needs to go through the original booking source. However, calling +1 - 866 - 226 - 7181 lets a Delta agent assess whether they can process the correction directly or need to coordinate with the agency on your behalf.

Will a name correction change my seat assignment or upgrades?

A minor name correction that does not require ticket reissuance generally preserves all seat assignments, upgrades, and ancillary purchases. If the ticket must be reissued, those may need to be reattached to the new ticket — confirm this with the Delta agent handling the correction before finalizing.

What if my name is spelled differently on my passport vs. my driver's license?

Use the document you will present at the airport and security checkpoint as the reference. For international flights, your passport is the controlling document. For domestic travel, either a passport or a REAL ID-compliant driver's license is accepted. Match the ticket name to whichever document you will actually use on travel day.
